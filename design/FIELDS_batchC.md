# Prismfall Field Compendium — Batch C (route, dungeon and story fields)

Same format as FIELDS.md (↑ boosted, ↓ weakened, ✕ fails, ⟳ transforms). Files 130–136.

## Cave — caves, mine tunnels and dungeons
*The cave walls loom close... every sound echoes.*
- ↑ Rock ×1.5. Damaging sound moves ×1.5 (they echo). Rock Climb ×1.5.
- ↓ Flying moves that don't make contact ×0.5 (no room to fly). ✕ Sky Drop.
- Sound-based status moves (Sing, Supersonic, Screech, Metal Sound…) ×1.3 accuracy. Flash drops accuracy one extra stage; Howl gives +1 Attack more.
- Stealth Rock hits twice as hard (Heavy-Duty Boots still protect).
- Underground: Sun, Rain and Hail can't start. Sandstorm lasts 3 turns longer.
- **Cave-in:** a quake move (Earthquake, Magnitude, Bulldoze, Fissure, Land's Wrath, Precipice Blades, Thousand Waves, Headlong Rush) cracks the ceiling. The next quake brings it down: every Pokémon takes 1/8 HP of Rock-type damage (scaled like Stealth Rock; Rock Head, Bulletproof and Overcoat are safe), and the cave settles again.
- Telluric Seed: +1 Defense and scatters Stealth Rock on the opposing side.
- Nature Power: Rock Tomb · Camouflage: Rock.

## Mountain — highland ridges and passes
*The wind howls across the mountain ridge!*
- ↑ Rock ×1.5, Flying ×1.2. Wind moves (Gust, Twister, Razor Wind, Air Cutter, Hurricane, Icy Wind, Ominous Wind, Silver Wind, Fairy Wind, Heat Wave, Petal Blizzard, Blizzard, and the four Storm moves) ×1.3.
- ↑ Avalanche ×1.5, Sky Attack ×1.2. Hurricane and the Storm moves ×1.2 accuracy.
- Tailwind lasts 2 turns longer. On entry: Wind Rider → +1 Attack; Wind Power → charged.
- Sunlight lasts 3 turns longer.
- ⟳ Hail, or 2 uses of Blizzard / Mountain Gale / Sheer Cold / Glaciate → **Snowy Mountain**.
- Elemental Seed: +1 Speed and whips up a Tailwind for the holder's side.
- Nature Power: Rock Slide · Camouflage: Rock.

## Snowy Mountain — Isenmar's frozen summits
*A blizzard rages over the snowbound peak!*
- ↑ Ice ×1.5, Rock ×1.2, Flying ×1.2; wind moves (as Mountain, plus Mountain Gale) ×1.3. ↓ Fire ×0.75.
- ↑ Powder Snow ×2; Avalanche, Ice Ball, Rollout ×1.5 (the snowball grows).
- Ice-type Pokémon take less from physical attacks (×1.5 Defense, as in snow).
- Blizzard never misses. Snow Cloak ×1.25 evasion and Slush Rush ×2 Speed without hail; Ice Body heals 1/16 each turn.
- On entry: Wind Rider → +1 Attack; Wind Power → charged. End of turn: the deep snow cures grounded Pokémon's burns.
- Hail lasts 3 turns longer.
- ⟳ Sun, or 2 uses of Heat Wave / Eruption / Lava Plume / Fire Pledge / Blast Burn / Magma Storm / Inferno / Overheat / Searing Shot / Blue Flare / Fusion Flare / Mind Blown → **Mountain**.
- Telluric Seed: +1 Defense and Defense Curl (pair it with Ice Ball or Rollout).
- Nature Power: Avalanche · Camouflage: Ice.

## Beach — the south coast around Ferrow's Landing
*Waves lap at the sandy shore...*
- ↑ Water ×1.2, Ground ×1.2. Mud-Slap ×2; Muddy Water, Sand Tomb ×1.5; Scorching Sands ×1.3 (50% burn chance).
- ↑ Tide-pool moves: Crabhammer, Razor Shell, Shell Side Arm ×1.3. Sand Force users' Ground/Rock/Steel moves ×1.3.
- Shore Up heals 2/3 of max HP. The sound of the waves: Calm Mind also gives +1 Sp. Atk, Meditate +1 Attack, and Kinesis drops accuracy one extra stage.
- Sand Attack never misses. Sand Veil ×1.2 evasion. Sand Rush and Swift Swim ×1.5 Speed without weather.
- On entry: Water Compaction → +1 Defense. End of turn: Water Absorb / Dry Skin heal 1/16; Hydration cures status.
- Hail can't form. Sun and Sandstorm last 2 turns longer.
- ⟳ Heavy rain, or 3 uses of Surf / Muddy Water / Water Spout / Origin Pulse / Water Pledge / Wave Crash → **Water Surface** (the tide comes in).
- Telluric Seed: +1 Defense and Aqua Ring.
- Nature Power: Muddy Water · Camouflage: Ground.

## Rainbow — story and legendary battles
*A brilliant rainbow arcs over the battlefield!*
- **Every move's secondary effect is twice as likely** (Serene Grace stacks).
- ↑ Normal ×1.5. ↓ Dark ×0.75 (the light washes out darkness).
- ↑ Light moves ×1.5: Aurora Beam, Signal Beam, Dazzling Gleam, Mist Ball, Luster Purge, Sparkling Aria, Silver Wind, Heart Stamp, Sacred Fire, Fire/Water/Grass Pledge. Prismatic Laser, Photon Geyser, Fleur Cannon ×1.3.
- Cosmic Power also gives +1 Sp. Def. Sleeping Pokémon recover 1/16 HP each turn.
- Misty Terrain lasts 3 turns longer.
- ⟳ Sandstorm, Hail or Defog → **no field** (the rainbow fades).
- Magical Seed: +1 Sp. Atk and +1 Sp. Def.
- Nature Power: Aurora Beam · Camouflage: Fairy.

## Wasteland — the Bleed-scarred badlands
*Poisoned slag and dead trees stretch to a jaundiced horizon...*
- ↑ Poison ×1.2. Sludge, Octazooka, Mud Bomb ×1.5; Gunk Shot, Belch ×1.3; Spit Up ×2. ↓ Grass ×0.75.
- ↑ Explosion, Self-Destruct, Mind Blown, Misty Explosion ×1.3 (trapped fumes).
- ✕ Synthesis, Morning Sun, Moonlight (no light to heal by).
- Hazards turn vicious: Stealth Rock also hits with an **Imprint shard** of a random type (1/16 HP, scaled by that type); Toxic Spikes also corrode grounded Pokémon for 1/16 HP (Poison-scaled). Heavy-Duty Boots protect.
- **Acid rain:** while it rains, Pokémon that aren't Poison or Steel lose 1/16 HP each turn (Overcoat protects). Poisoned Poison Heal users heal 1/16 more.
- Sandstorm lasts 3 turns longer.
- ⟳ Grassy Terrain → **Flower Garden** (life returns). 2 uses of Purify → **no field**. (A burning Corrosive Mire becomes the Wasteland.)
- Telluric Seed: +1 Attack and +1 Sp. Atk, but badly poisons the holder (great on Poison types and Poison Heal).
- Nature Power: Gunk Shot · Camouflage: Poison.

## Prism Arena — Crownhaven, the Festival of Champions finale
*The eighteen Plates blaze in a ring above Crownhaven's arena!*
- **Opening:** for the first 3 turns the Plates rest (no gym field is active).
- **The Plates turn:** every 3 turns the arena takes on the full effects of the next gym field (boosts, nerfs, failing moves, end-of-turn effects, weather/terrain rules). Borrowed fields never transform the arena and their seeds don't react.
- **Always announced:** one turn before each change, the next Plate begins to glow and the coming field is named.
- **Fixed order** (then it loops): Volcanic Field (Fire) → Water Surface → Flower Garden → Neon Grid → Frozen Lake → Colosseum → Desert → Corrosive Mire → Stormfront → Mindscape → Silkwood → Crystal Cavern → Hollow Cathedral → New Moon → Fairy Tale → Factory → Dragon's Den → Grand Stage (Normal). No two neighbours share a weakness, so one counter never covers two turns.
- The type of the current field is **ascendant**: Multitype Pokémon become that type, and Judgment strikes with it.
- **Dissonance:** super-effective hits on Pokémon of the ascendant type ×1.2. **Resonance:** a Pokémon holding the ascendant type's Plate heals 1/16 HP each turn.
- ↑ Tri Attack ×1.3, Prismatic Laser ×1.2.
- Magical Seed: +1 Sp. Atk and +1 Sp. Def.
- Nature Power: Judgment (takes the ascendant type) · Camouflage: Normal.
