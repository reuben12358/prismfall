# Prismfall Field Compendium (player-facing)

Field effects are the land's Imprints at work. A field lasts the whole battle unless something transforms it.
Built-in terrains (Electric/Grassy/Misty/Psychic) stack on top of a field unless the field says otherwise.
**Seeds**: Elemental, Magical, Telluric and Synthetic Seeds activate once when their holder enters a field that reacts to
them (or when the field appears mid-battle), then are used up.

In-game, **Field Notes** (pause menu) records every field you battle on and shows a summary; unseen fields read "???".

Legend: ↑ boosted, ↓ weakened, ✕ fails, ⟳ transforms into another field.

## Silkwood — Gym 1 (Bug), Briarwick
*Giant webs glisten between the ancient trees.*
- ↑ Bug ×1.5, Fire ×1.3 (the silk burns), Grass ×1.2. Swarm is always active.
- ↑ Electroweb, Struggle Bug ×1.5; Lunge, Leech Life, Pounce, Attack Order ×1.3. Cutting moves (Cut, Fury Cutter, X-Scissor, Slash, Night Slash, Leaf Blade, Sacred Sword, Air Slash, Psycho Cut, Cross Poison) ×1.2.
- ↓ Flying moves from non-Bug users ×0.8 (snagged in webs).
- Grounded non-Bug Pokémon lose 1 Speed the first time they enter the battle (Heavy-Duty Boots avoid it).
- Bug contact moves: 20% to lower Speed. Bug moves' own secondary effects are twice as likely.
- String Shot / Electroweb / Sticky Web never miss; String Shot & Silk Trap lower Speed one extra stage; Defend Order gives +1 Def more. Powder moves ×1.2 accuracy; Compound Eyes ×1.1 more.
- Bug types recover 1/16 HP each turn. Grassy Terrain lasts 3 turns longer.
- ⟳ 3 damaging Fire moves → **Ashen Waste** (the forest burns down).
- Telluric Seed: +1 Defense and Ingrain.
- Nature Power: Bug Buzz · Camouflage: Bug.

## Grand Stage — Gym 2 (Normal), Saltmere
*The curtain rises! The spotlight blazes over the stage!*
- Indoors: Sun, Rain, Sandstorm and Hail cannot start.
- ↑ Normal ×1.5. Sound moves ×1.5 (Punk Rock / Liquid Voice users ×1.2 more). Damaging Dance moves ×1.5.
- ↑ Pay Day ×2, Acrobatics ×1.5, Drum Beating ×1.5, Fake Out ×1.5, Last Resort ×1.3.
- Damaging Sound moves: 10% to confuse. Sing, Grass Whistle, Supersonic, Metal Sound ×1.3 accuracy.
- Status Dance moves also give +1 Speed. Celebrate raises every stat. Charm / Captivate / Tearful Look also drop Sp. Atk.
- On entry: Dancer → +1 Speed; Punk Rock / Liquid Voice → +1 Sp. Atk.
- Magical Seed: +1 Speed and Laser Focus (next hit crits).
- Nature Power: Hyper Voice · Camouflage: Normal.

## Water Surface — Gym 3 (Water), Ebbmouth
*The battle is taking place on the open water!*
- ↑ Water ×1.5 (Torrent always active). Electric ×1.5 against grounded targets. ↓ Fire ×0.5.
- ↑ Whirlpool ×2 (and ×1.25 accuracy), Water Pledge ×1.5, Dive ×1.3, Muddy Water / Surging Strikes ×1.2.
- ✕ Earthquake, Magnitude, Fissure, Bulldoze, Dig, Stomping Tantrum, Spikes, Toxic Spikes, Stealth Rock.
- Grounded non-Water Pokémon move at ×0.75 Speed; Swift Swim ×1.5 Speed even without rain.
- Damaging Water moves: 20% to lower a grounded non-Water target's Speed.
- End of turn: grounded burned Pokémon are cured; Hydration cures status; Water Absorb / Dry Skin heal 1/16.
- Steam Engine / Water Compaction: +1 Defense on entry. Rain lasts 3 turns longer. Grassy Terrain can't form.
- ⟳ Dive → **Underwater**. 3 uses of Blizzard / Sheer Cold / Glaciate / Ice Beam / Freeze-Dry → **Frozen Lake**.
- Elemental Seed: +1 Speed and Aqua Ring.
- Nature Power: Whirlpool · Camouflage: Water.

## Underwater
*Blue silence presses in from every side.*
- ↑ Water ×1.5, Electric ×1.3, Sound moves ×1.3. ↓ Flying ×0.5. Physical non-Water moves from non-Water users ×0.5.
- ↑ Dive, Anchor Shot, Whirlpool ×1.5; Water Pulse ×1.3; Crabhammer ×1.2. Whirlpool ×1.25 accuracy.
- ✕ All Fire moves. ✕ Fly, Bounce, Sky Drop, Spikes, Toxic Spikes, Stealth Rock, Sticky Web.
- Non-Water Pokémon move at half Speed (Swift Swim: double Speed).
- End of turn: non-Water Pokémon lose 1/16 HP scaled by Water effectiveness (Fire/Rock/Ground lose 1/8); Water Absorb / Dry Skin / Storm Drain / Swift Swim / Water Veil are safe. Water Absorb / Dry Skin heal 1/16.
- Burns are washed away on entry. No weather or terrain can exist.
- ⟳ Dive or Bounce → back to **Water Surface**.
- Elemental Seed: +1 Defense and Aqua Ring.
- Nature Power: Water Pulse · Camouflage: Water.

## Neon Grid — Gym 4 (Electric), Lumen City (Zenith)
*Neon circuitry hums beneath your feet!*
- ↑ Electric ×1.5, Steel ×1.2. Pulse moves ×1.5. Light moves (Dazzling Gleam, Flash Cannon, Light of Ruin, Prismatic Laser, Photon Geyser, Luster Purge, Mirror Shot, Signal Beam, Techno Blast) ×1.3.
- ↑ Rising Voltage ×1.5; Discharge, Parabolic Charge ×1.3.

- On entry: Plus / Minus → +1 Sp. Atk; Motor Drive / Lightning Rod / Volt Absorb / Electromorphosis → charged.
- End of turn: grounded non-Electric, non-Ground Pokémon have a 10% chance to be paralyzed; Volt Absorb heals 1/16. Electric Terrain lasts 3 turns longer.
- ⟳ 2 uses of Earthquake / Magnitude / Bulldoze / Fissure / Precipice Blades / Thousand Waves → the grid shorts out (**no field**).
- Synthetic Seed: +1 Sp. Atk and Magnet Rise (floats above the stray currents).
- Nature Power: Discharge · Camouflage: Electric.

## Desert — Gym 5 (Ground), Duneholt
*Heat shimmers over the endless dunes.*
- ↑ Ground ×1.2, Fire ×1.2. ↓ Ice ×0.75, Water ×0.8.
- ↑ Sand Tomb ×2; Scorching Sands, Solar Beam, Solar Blade, Needle Arm, Dig ×1.5; Sandsear Storm, Thousand Waves, Bone Club, Bonemerang, Bone Rush ×1.3.

## Corrosive Mire — Gym 6 (Poison), Mirefen
*Toxic sludge bubbles underfoot.*
- ↑ Poison ×1.5 (its secondary effects ×1.5 more likely). ↓ Grass ×0.75. **Poison moves hit Steel types** (neutral).
- ↑ Acid, Acid Spray, Smog ×2; Clear Smog, Mud Bomb, Mud Shot, Mud-Slap ×1.5; Sludge Wave ×1.3. Mud moves and Muddy Water also hit as Poison.
- Gunk Shot ×1.25 accuracy, Smog / Poison Gas ×1.4. Damaging Water moves: 20% to poison.
- On entry: Merciless / Corrosion / Poison Touch → +1 Attack.
- End of turn: Poison types heal 1/16; grounded Steel types corrode for 1/16; other grounded non-Poison Pokémon have a 10% chance to be poisoned.
- **Fire ignites the fumes**: every damaging Fire move causes an explosion hitting all non-Fire Pokémon for 1/8 HP.
- ⟳ Misty Terrain, or Purify ×2 → **Water Surface** (purified). 2 damaging Fire moves → **Wasteland**.
- Synthetic Seed: poisons the holder but gives +1 Attack and +1 Sp. Atk (great with Guts, Poison Heal, Toxic Boost).
- Nature Power: Acid Spray · Camouflage: Poison.

## Volcanic Field — Gym 7 (Fire), Cindervault
*Magma churns across the battlefield!*
- ↑ Fire ×1.5 (Blaze always active). ↓ Ice ×0.5. Damaging Rock moves also hit as Fire.
- ↑ Lava Plume, Flame Burst, Fire Spin, Smog, Clear Smog ×1.5; Magma Storm, Earth Power, Scald, Steam Eruption ×1.3.

- End of turn: grounded non-Fire Pokémon lose 1/16 HP scaled by Fire effectiveness (Flash Fire, Magma Armor, Water Veil, Heatproof, Well-Baked Body are safe). Frozen Pokémon thaw. Magma Armor heals 1/16.

- Hail can't form; Sun lasts 3 turns longer; Grassy and Misty Terrain burn away.
- ⟳ 2 uses of Surf / Muddy Water / Water Spout / Hydro Pump / Water Pledge / Sparkling Aria / Soak, or Rain → **Ashen Waste**.
- Elemental Seed: burns the holder but gives +1 Attack, +1 Sp. Atk, +1 Speed.
- Nature Power: Lava Plume · Camouflage: Fire.

## Ashen Waste
*Grey ash drifts over the cooled crust.*
- ↑ Ground, Rock, Grass ×1.2 (fertile ash). ↓ Fire ×0.8.
- ↑ Sand Tomb, Mud-Slap ×1.5; Scorching Sands ×1.3. Wind moves (Gust, Twister, Hurricane, Air Cutter, Razor Wind, Bleakwind Storm) ×1.2 and 30% to lower accuracy.
- Moves from non-Ground/Rock/Fire users have ×0.9 accuracy; Sand Veil ×1.25 evasion; Sand Rush ×1.5 Speed.
- Sand Attack / Smokescreen drop accuracy one extra stage; Growth also gives +1 Sp. Atk.
- End of turn: Pokémon that aren't Fire/Ground/Rock/Steel lose 1/16 HP (Overcoat, Sand Veil, Sand Rush, Sand Force, Safety Goggles protect).
- Sandstorm and Grassy Terrain last 2 turns longer.
- ⟳ Defog → **no field**. 3 uses of Eruption / Lava Plume / Magma Storm / Fire Pledge → **Volcanic Field**.
- Telluric Seed: +1 Defense and +1 evasion.
- Nature Power: Scorching Sands · Camouflage: Ground.

## Inverse Field — Antumbra, the Kingdom Behind the Glass
*The world turns inside out... Type matchups are reversed!*
- **Inverse type chart**: immunities and resistances become weaknesses (2×); weaknesses become resistances (0.5×). Airborne Pokémon still dodge Ground moves.
- ↑ Ghost, Psychic, Dark ×1.2. Shadow Force, Mirror Shot ×1.5; Phantom Force, Foul Play ×1.3; Psyshock ×1.2.
- Confuse Ray, Hypnosis, Dark Void ×1.2 accuracy. Topsy-Turvy also gives the user +1 Speed.
- Trick Room, Magic Room and Wonder Room last 3 turns longer. Psychic Terrain lasts 3 turns longer.
- Contrary Pokémon "feel at home" on entry (−1 Speed, which Contrary turns into +1).
- Magical Seed: −2 Speed, +1 Attack, +1 Sp. Atk (pair it with Trick Room).
- Nature Power: Tri Attack · Camouflage: Ghost.

## The Bleed
*Reality frays at the edges... The Bleed is spreading!*
- Every 3 turns (and immediately at the start) it **borrows the effects of another field** at random — boosts, nerfs, failing moves, end-of-turn effects, weather/terrain rules and all. Borrowed fields never transform the Bleed and their seeds don't react.
- Attacks that don't match the user's type are ×1.2 (raw Imprint energy rewards coverage).
- End of turn: each Pokémon has a 20% chance to have its power warped: one random stat +1, another −1.
- Magical Seed: a random stat +2. Synthetic Seed: +1 Defense and +1 Sp. Def.
- Nature Power: Tri Attack · Camouflage: Psychic.
