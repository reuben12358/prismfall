# CHAPTER BEATS — the one or two key events every chapter must land

> User (2026-11-21): *"try not to make each chapter thin please... we want every chapter to be significant to some extent with either 1 or 2 key events happening each chapter."*
> Owner: story editor. Sources: `story/04_ACT_I.md` – `08_ACT_V.md`, `09_MYSTERIES_FORESHADOWING.md`, `10_CHOICES_ENDINGS.md`, `14_WAR_AND_BLESSING.md`. This file adds no keys; it says which beats carry each chapter so scripts, side quests and maps give them room.
> **A key event** is a story turn the player will remember: a reveal, a betrayal, a disaster, a choice with consequences, a big boss or legendary encounter, a character arc landing. A gym or a routine rival fight never counts on its own.
> **How to use it.** Script writers: each key event needs build-up on screen before it, a payoff you can see, and a consequence that shows up later (an NPC line, a map state, a choice key). Side-quest and map builders: don't put a sidequest, joke or optional zone *inside* a key event (15_COMEDY §1), and keep the maps it happens on free of clutter at that spot.

**Status:** ● strong (two real key events in the outline, or a strengthening the creator approved) · ◐ thin in the outline, strengthened here (the script must deliver the proposal) · ○ thin, proposal needs the creator's yes. After the creator's decisions of 2026-11-21 every chapter is ●.

## Proposals for the thin chapters

### A. Chapter 4, "Both Ways" — make the night one event, and give Larch's confession a scene (**approved**)
**What the outline had:** K2, a Hollow cell spiking the Lattice, a child's boat, Teo running, a three-way choice, and Larch's "It's my design" as a line after the gym. All the pieces, but spread thin, and Larch's line read as a status update.
**The strengthening (no new keys, no new characters):**
1. **Build-up.** Teo is met by day first, as Marisol's charming, furious little brother at the strike vote on the Mill Quay, heckling his sister. The river stutters three times during the day, each time longer. Kestrel is hunting "Thistle's people" and swears the cell is in Ebbmouth.
2. **The night.** At midnight Meridian's tidal Lattice runs its night export draw, and the Ebb stops dead for an hour (V108 = 2), longer than it ever has. A girl takes a punt out on the dead-still river to fetch her toy boat. The masked cell blows the Lattice intake to shut the draw, and the river comes back all at once, recklessly, because nobody looked at the water: the punt is dragged at the Guild mill-race, the race that killed the Reyes father in AF 297. Battle-and-chase on the towpath (the cell, then the masked leader), then the PC, Marisol and the masked boy haul the race gate down together. In the lamplight his mask is pushed up: it's Teo. Marisol lets him run.
3. **The choice in public.** A Meridian guard arrives and asks the PC who it was, with Marisol standing right there (`:ch4_teo`: report / keep_secret / confront her privately afterwards).
4. **The morning after.** The consequence is on the quay before the gym: wanted posters for Teo (report), or Marisol's mother's door open (keep_secret / confront).
5. **Larch's confession as its own scene** (after the gym): she lays the tide tables on the Pokémon Center counter. The stutters line up with Meridian's draw, and the full stop lines up with the *night export*. Meridian's draw is the cause; the Hollow's sabotage brought the river back, recklessly, with a child on the water. Both are true, and that is what gives `:ch4_teo` its weight. "It's not the Hollow stopping the river. It's us. It's my design. I lit up this whole region, you know. Eleven cities on my design." It's the first time the PC learns their mentor built the machine, and it seeds the red herring that she works for Meridian (resolved Ch12).
**Why it doesn't break later beats:** Teo is still only an Ebbmouth cell leader, so his Ch9 fate is the same choice with more weight. The Hollow being partly right is the Act I thesis. Larch's secret was always revealed here; only the staging grows.

### B. Chapter 7, "The Quiet Ward" — turn Sable's broadcast into the night the Ward nearly falls (**approved**)
**What the outline had:** J2 and the ledger (strong), Wick (good), Sweetrot (texture), and Sable's broadcast as a cliffhanger. The chapter has one key event and a lot of mood.
**The strengthening (uses existing cast and threads; no new keys):**
1. The night of Sable's broadcast, the Hammer cuts the Infirmary's power from the Gutterfall junction (the Ward's dampers run on Zenith current). The broadcast plays on the Ward's own screen as the lights die: *"The Mourner's gone soft. The Hammer hasn't."*
2. Without the dampers, the Quiet Ward's Bleed rises into a Veil. Corvin and Wenlow can't go in. The PC can. They carry out the salt-haired child (the patient Wenlow introduced by name earlier in the chapter), and under the glass the child's eyes are moving. Corvin, on the roof afterwards: "They're not dying. They're *dreaming*." (09 #21, now seen, not just told.)
3. Juno helps restart the dampers with her seed-medicine stills; it's the last time in Act II she's luminous with purpose before Ironreach.
4. Consequence: the Hammer is now a named enemy of the Hollow's own people, and Wick's "he wants to carry them home" lands as a schism, not a sermon. The Ward is the place the Purge goes for in Ch16, so the player already knows every bed.
**Doesn't break:** no one dies (Wenlow's death stays in Ch16), the child stays Fixed (the Homecoming waking is unchanged), and the PC's immunity is something everyone has already seen at the Lowlight.
**Staging:** a scripted Bleed set piece to Mirefen, staged on the Infirmary's own Quiet Ward (map 223's top floor; maps.json "Quiet Ward on the highest stilts"), not the gym's Mastery room 234. **Approved 2026-11-21:** it's canon in `story/26_SCRIPT_CH7.md` and `05_ACT_II.md`; the old roof-only version is retired.

## Notes for the other workstreams
- **Side quests:** a chapter's optional content should sit *around* its key events, never in the hour between the build-up and the payoff (e.g. nothing posted on the Ebbmouth board that sends the player away between the strike vote and the night; nothing new in the Lowlight after the Gala starts).
- **Map builders:** every key event above needs room on its map: the Guild mill-race and towpath on 160 (Ch4), the Lowlight 181 → 608 swap with twelve figures at the positions of twelve living NPCs (Ch5). Those lists are in each script's "Map events needed".
- **Densest chapters** (13, 14, 20): keep their gyms and side content short so the key events breathe.
