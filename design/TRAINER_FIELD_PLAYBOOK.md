# Trainer Field Playbook

How every important opponent uses their field (SPINE Revision 10, item 4). For each one: the field, the game plan, the
transition play, how they answer the obvious flip, and what the player is meant to do about it.

- Data: `pbs/trainers.txt`, generated by `tools/trainer_gen/gen.py` (+ `routes.py`, `cameos.py`). Edit the generator, not the PBS.
- Field rules: `design/FIELDS.md`. Level curve and difficulty tiers: `design/BALANCE.md`.
- Sets below are **Standard**. Hard adds a Pokémon and sharper sets; Casual drops one Pokémon and the nastiest tool
  (usually the seed, the weather or the transition move).
- Check any time: `ruby tools/trainer_gen/dump_fields.rb && python3 -I tools/trainer_gen/field_fit.py --all`
  (lists each team's field hooks and fails on a move or ability the field switches off).

"Transform strike" = the move that completes a field change hits 1.3× harder. A "stage play" changes a stage inside
the same field (no strike unless the field says so).

## 2. Early game (Chapters 0–2, Gyms 1–3)

Design rule for this stretch: one new field idea per fight, the Gym Guide's advice must work, and the Warden's
counter to that advice is a single Pokémon the player can remove.

### Pallbearer Thistle — Silkwood (Briarwick, before Gym 1)
Morelull 13, Foongus 14, Phantump "Lowmarsh" 15.
- **Plan.** Grass ×1.2, powders ×1.2 accuracy (Stun Spore), Leech Seed and drain moves outlast a team that has
  just walked through sticky silk (−1 Speed on first entry).
- **Transition.** She cannot start one (no Fire moves at this level) and does not need to: if the player burns the
  wood, the Wildfire burns down to Embers after 4 rounds, and Embers give Grass and Ghost ×1.2 and heal Grass types.
  Ingrain, Growth and Leech Seed (any two) then regrow the ash into a Meadow. "Let the land grieve."
- **Counterplay.** Fire and Flying; she has no answer to a fast Fire type. This is the first Silkwood fight, so it is
  deliberately mild.

### Gym 1: Tamsin — Silkwood (cap 15)
Joltik 13, Surskit 13 (not on Casual), Swadloon "Bobbin" 15. Hard adds Vivillon.
- **Plan.** Speed control, then Bobbin outlasts. Electroweb is ×1.5, never misses and paralyzes 30% of the time;
  String Shot never misses and drops Speed three stages; Bug moves are ×1.5 and Bug types heal 1/16 a turn.
- **Seed.** Bobbin holds the **Telluric Seed** (+1 Def, +1 Sp. Def, −1 Speed: "a silk cocoon"). It is the first seed
  the player sees used against them; Ranger Ash in the gym shows one a room earlier.
- **Transition.** None of her own: Silkwood's only change is being burned, and she will not burn her grandmother's
  room.
- **Answer to the flip.** Three Fire attacks set the wood ablaze. Surskit's Rain Dance halves Fire, doubles its own
  Speed (Swift Swim), makes Electric ×1.2 on the wet silk, and douses a Wildfire to Embers.
- **Counterplay.** Fire still wins (it one-shots Surskit and is 4× on Bobbin); knock Surskit out before it rains, or
  wait five turns. Non-Bug fliers are ×0.8 here, so the Gym Guide's "Fire and Flying" is half right on purpose.
- Standard changes from the old team: seed instead of Eviolite (Eviolite stays on Hard), Rain Dance on Surskit.

### Gym 2: Dorian — Grand Stage, double battle (cap 19)
Chatot 17, Ambipom 18, Loudred 18 (not on Casual), Kecleon 19. Hard adds Furfrou.
- **Plan.** Play to the crowd. Applause rises a stage for every two sound moves, two showpieces (Fake Out, Encore)
  or any critical hit, and each stage raises Normal and sound damage. Chatot's Chatter and Round, Ambipom's
  Technician Fake Out (×1.5 here) and Swift, Loudred's Echoed Voice.
- **Seed.** Kecleon holds the **Synthetic Seed**: +1 Speed and Laser Focus (its first hit is a sure crit, which is
  another applause stage) at the cost of being Taunted, and its set is four attacks.
- **Stage play.** Fake Out + Encore on turn one is two showpieces: straight to Encore.
- **Answer to the flip.** The player lowers the applause with Protect, Recover or Roost. Chatot's Encore (it lasts
  two turns longer here) locks them into the stalling move, which then fails.
- **Counterplay.** Fighting types, Soundproof, and stalling moves to keep the stage low. Loudred is Scrappy, so a
  Ghost is not a free switch.
- Standard is softer than before: Loudred lost Uproar, and the Chatot + Loudred Round duet (a one-hit KO at this
  level) is Hard-only.

### Gym 3: Marisol — Water Surface (cap 23)
Pelipper 21, Lanturn 22 (not on Casual), Floatzel 22, Gyarados "Lucky" 23. Hard adds Araquanid.
- **Plan.** Water ×1.5, Swift Swim works without rain, and the player's grounded non-Water Pokémon wade at ×0.75
  Speed. Floatzel is the sweeper.
- **Seed.** Lanturn holds the **Elemental Seed** (+1 Speed, Aqua Ring).
- **Transition play.** Lucky's **Dive** drags the fight Underwater with a transform strike: "a current that changes
  direction mid-fight". Underwater suits the whole team (non-Water Pokémon move at half Speed and their physical
  hits are halved). It is on the ace only, so it happens at the end, and Dive gives the player a free turn.
- **Answer to the flip.** The obvious answers are Electric and Grass. Lanturn absorbs Electric and its own Spark is
  ×1.5 against grounded targets; Pelipper's Flying moves and Lucky's Ice Fang cover Grass.
- **Counterplay.** Electric from a Pokémon that is not grounded-and-slow, or Grass; underwater, Electric is ×1.5 and
  never misses, which is 4× on Lucky. Hazards and Ground moves fail on the water.
- Standard no longer has Drizzle: rain stacked on the field made Water Pulse a one-hit KO in the harness. Hard keeps
  Drizzle + Damp Rock (rain lasts 8 + 3 turns here).

### J1: Juno — Meadow (Saltmere promenade green, cap 19)
Cottonee 15 (not on Casual), Bounsweet "Sweetpea" 15, starter 17. Hard adds Petilil.
- **Plan.** Fairy Wind and Razor Leaf are ×1.5 in the Meadow breeze; Leaf Guard is always on.
- **Transition play.** A Prankster **Grassy Terrain** turns the Meadow into a Flower Garden at once, the field she
  will defend at Gym 9, and the terrain lasts 8 turns there.
- **Counterplay.** Two Fire attacks burn a Meadow (one in sun); this is the friendliest place to learn that.

### Teo — Water Surface (Ebbmouth, before Gym 3)
Carvanha 19 (not on Casual), Arrokuda 20, Chewtle "Snapper" 21. Hard adds Basculin.
- **Plan.** A Swift Swim rush (Arrokuda, and Snapper now has Swift Swim too) with Water ×1.5.
- **Transition.** None, on purpose: this fight introduces the field one battle before the gym teaches its changes.
- **Counterplay.** Electric and Grass, as for Marisol, without Lanturn in the way.

### K2: Kestrel — Mountain (Route 6 cliffs, cap 23)
Staravia 19 (not on Casual), Honedge "Morwen" 19, starter 21. Hard adds Sandile.
- **Plan.** Flying ×1.2 (Wing Attack, Aerial Ace on both). Staravia's **Telluric Seed** gives +1 Speed and a Tailwind
  for his side: "I don't do fair fights."
- **Counterplay.** Rock is ×1.5 here and Thunder never misses on the peaks; stall the Tailwind out with Protect.

## 3. Gyms 4–18

### Gym 4: Rook — Neon Grid (27)
Luxio, Electabuzz, Toxtricity, Rotom-Wash "Camera".
- **Plan.** Electric ×1.5. Toxtricity's Eerie Impulse drops Sp. Atk three stages here; Camera's Hydro Pump carries
  the grid's 30% paralysis, which feeds its Hex.
- **Seed.** Electabuzz: **Synthetic Seed** (+1 Sp. Atk and Magnet Rise).
- **Stage play / answer to the flip.** The player blacks the grid out with two quakes or two floods. Charge (Luxio,
  Electabuzz, Toxtricity) reboots it in two uses and gives +1 Sp. Atk here; Luxio's Bite is his hedge while it is
  dark (Dark ×1.3). Electabuzz floats over the quakes and Camera levitates.
- **Counterplay.** Ground types, then keep the grid dark: Electric damage swings between ×0.5 and ×2 in a fixed
  cycle that can be read. Camera's own Hydro Pump counts toward a Blackout.

### Gym 5: Sahar — Desert (31)
Hippopotas, Sandaconda, Krokorok, Claydol "Effigy".
- **Plan.** Sand Tomb ×2, Dig ×1.5, Ground types take ×0.67 from special attacks, sandstorm lasts 8 + 3 turns.
- **Seed.** Sandaconda: **Telluric Seed**.
- **Stage play / answer to the flip.** Rain or three floods wake a Superbloom (Grass ×1.5). Sand Stream, Sand Spit
  (it triggers when the Water move lands) and two of Krokorok's Fire Fangs each bake it back to dunes.
- **Counterplay.** Grass, with rain timed after Hippopotas and Sandaconda are down. Water is ×0.8 here.
- She has no cross-field transition (Cinderglass needs searing Fire moves her team does not learn).

### Gym 6: Corvin — Corrosive Mire (35, double in the story)
Galarian Weezing, Crobat, Amoonguss, Drapion, Toxapex "Ward".
- **Plan.** Poison ×1.5 and it hits Steel; Venoshock is always doubled against grounded targets; Poison types
  fling poison each turn and heal 1/16. Ward's Merciless gets +1 Attack on entry and crits whatever is poisoned.
- **Seed.** Crobat: **Synthetic Seed** (+1 Atk, +1 Sp. Atk, holder poisoned; a Poison type cannot be poisoned).
- **Transition play.** Weezing's **Heat Wave** ignites the marsh gas (1/8 to the player's grounded non-Fire Pokémon
  per use) and the second one burns it off to a Wasteland with a transform strike. Quakes are halved there, which
  blunts the Ground answer; his team keeps Poison ×1.2 and Toxic Boost.
- **Answer to the flip.** The player's Fire does the same thing to him; Ward's Scald answers Fire types. Weezing
  never has Misty Surge (Misty Terrain purifies the mire).
- **Counterplay.** Ground and Psychic before the Wasteland; Misty Terrain or two Purifies to wash the field away.

### Gym 7: Brann — Volcanic (39)
Torkoal, Camerupt, Magmortar, Arcanine, Coalossal "Anvil".
- **Plan.** Lava Plume and Earth Power prime an eruption at the end of the round: his Fire types get +1, lava
  rains on the player's grounded Pokémon, hazards are blown away. Drought halves Water.
- **Seed.** Camerupt: **Elemental Seed** (+1 Atk, Sp. Atk and Speed; the burn cannot touch a Fire type). It also
  has Magma Armor now (+1 Def on entry, 1/16 a turn, +1 Def/Sp. Def per eruption).
- **Transition play.** After a quench, three Lava Plumes (Torkoal, Camerupt) re-melt Cinderglass to Volcanic.
- **Answer to the flip.** The quench is the intended answer, so Anvil is built for Cinderglass: Steam Engine gains
  Speed every turn there and Rock Slide is ×1.3 on obsidian.
- **Counterplay.** Rain Dance is the best single move in this fight: it replaces the sun and quenches the field at
  once. Otherwise two Surfs, with Torkoal removed first.
- Removed: Stealth Rock on Torkoal and Coalossal (his own eruptions blew it away) and Yawn (eruptions wake sleepers).

### Gym 8: Kaito — Colosseum (43)
Hawlucha, Machamp, Hitmontop, Conkeldurr, Lucario "Memory".
- **Plan.** Punches and kicks ×1.2 on top of Fighting ×1.2; every knockout gives +1 Attack; two knockouts start a
  Frenzy (+1 crit stage for everything).
- **Seed.** Hawlucha: **Synthetic Seed** (+1 Attack, Focus Energy, Taunted). Eating it triggers Unburden and powers
  Acrobatics up; the set is four attacks.
- **Abilities.** Machamp's No Guard is +1 Attack on entry; Memory's Inner Focus is +1 crit stage, with a Scope Lens.
- **Stage play.** The Frenzy comes from knockouts. Nobody on the team uses a quake (three would drop the pit into a Cave).
- **Counterplay.** Two stalling moves calm a Frenzy. Psychic (special Psychic is booed, ×0.8), Flying, Fairy,
  Intimidate; burns except on Conkeldurr.

### Gym 9: Juno (J3) — Flower Garden (47)
Whimsicott, Shiinotic, Lilligant, starter, Tsareena "Sweetpea".
- **Plan.** Grow the garden: Whimsicott's **Telluric Seed** (+1 stage on entry, Ingrain, +1 Sp. Def), Prankster
  Growth and Leech Seed, Lilligant's Aromatherapy, and every second Grass attack. Petal Dance is a petal move and
  Own Tempo never tires of it.
- **Answer to the flip.** Fire prunes the garden a stage per hit; her answer is regrowth speed. At stage 5 the
  overgrowth makes Grass weaknesses neutral, but two Fire attacks then set it ablaze. If that happens, Growth and
  Leech Seed turn the Embers back into a Meadow.
- **Counterplay.** Hold Fire until stage 5 and burn it down, or cut it back early with Fire and blades. Acid moves
  reset it to stage 1. The harness challenger won this way in 8 turns: the garden is strong and flammable by design.

### Gym 10: Anja — Frozen Lake (51)
Alolan Ninetales, Cloyster, Beartic, Walrein, Avalugg, Mamoswine "Matriarch".
- **Plan.** Ice ×1.5, Slush Rush without hail, Aurora Veil without hail, priority and Ice Shard slide for +1 Speed.
- **Seed.** Matriarch: **Elemental Seed** (+2 Speed for 1/8 HP).
- **Transition play.** Avalugg's **Body Press** and Matriarch's **High Horsepower** are heavy blows; three crack the
  lake to open water with a transform strike. That halves the challenger's Fire moves and feeds Walrein's Surf and
  Cloyster's Razor Shell.
- **Answer to the flip.** Melting the lake is what a Fire team does anyway, so the destination is covered above.
  Each of her Ice attacks refreezes one crack, and Blizzard / Freeze-Dry refreeze open water in three.
- **Counterplay.** Fighting and Steel rather than Fire; Electric and Grass once it is water.
- Matriarch uses High Horsepower instead of Earthquake: quakes lay Spikes on both sides here and fail on open water.

### Gym 11: Odessa — Stormfront (55, double in the story)
Pelipper, Swanna, Pom-Pom Oricorio, Noivern, Kilowattrel, Corviknight "Taxi".
- **Plan.** Hurricane and Thunder never miss; Tailwind lasts two turns longer. Kilowattrel's Wind Power arrives
  charged; Swanna's Big Pecks is +1 Defense on entry.
- **Seed.** Oricorio: **Elemental Seed** (+1 Speed and Charge, so the first Electric Revelation Dance is doubled).
- **Transition play.** Noivern's **Sunny Day** twice tears the storm open into a Rainbow: added effects are doubled
  (Air Slash flinches 60%, Hurricane confuses 60%) and Normal is ×1.5 for Hyper Voice. No strike: it is a status move.
- **Answer to the flip.** Electric and Rock are the answers. Kilowattrel replaces Talonflame as the Electric check;
  Taxi's Iron Head handles Rock and Ice.
- **Counterplay.** Electric is ×1.3 here for the player too; Smack Down is ×2. Defog brings the storm back.
- No Drizzle any more: in rain the Stormfront's lightning strikes every airborne non-Electric Pokémon, which was her
  own team.

### Gym 12: Ilsa — Mindscape (59)
Bronzong, Musharna, Espeon, Gothitelle, Reuniclus, Hatterene "Hush".
- **Plan.** Trick Room (it lasts 7 turns here), Psychic ×1.5.
- **Transition play.** Hush's **Psychic Noise** is a damaging sound move: the third one cracks the dream into the
  Inverse Field with a transform strike. There the Dark, Ghost and Bug answers are resisted, Psychic hits Dark
  types for double, and Trick Room lasts 8 turns. Lucent sits in the Reverse Kingdom; this is home.
- **Answer to the flip.** Three Dark attacks curdle the dream into a Nightmare (Dark ×1.5). Two Calm Minds
  (Musharna, Espeon, Gothitelle) soothe it back; Reuniclus's Focus Blast is for the Dark types.
- **Counterplay.** Dark and Ghost early, Taunt on the Trick Room setters, and Topsy-Turvy rights an inverted field.
- Psychic Noise is not in the base learnset data (it is a Scarlet/Violet TM); it is on the validator allowlist.

### Gym 13: Bastian — Crystal Cavern (63)
Carbink, Alolan Golem, Aurorus, Dusk Lycanroc, Rhyperior, Gigalith "Geode".
- **Plan.** Light moves ×1.5 (Power Gem, Dazzling Gleam, Flash Cannon, Ancient Power), Rock ×1.3, screens last two
  turns longer.
- **Seed.** Geode: **Magical Seed** (+1 Sp. Atk and a Light Screen, 7 turns here) against Water and Grass specials.
- **Transition play.** A miner's blast: Golem's Galvanized **Explosion** shatters the crystals into a Cave with a
  transform strike. Rock is ×1.5 there, Stealth Rock cuts twice as deep and Aurorus's Hyper Voice echoes (×1.5).
  Three Earthquakes do the same more slowly.
- **Answer to the flip.** One Dark Pulse darkens the cavern. Two light moves relight it (Carbink, Aurorus, Geode).
- **Counterplay.** Water, Grass, Fighting, Steel; darken the cave to strip the light boosts.
- Geode lost Sand Stream: a sandstorm blots out his own crystals.

### Gym 14: Ysolde — Hollow Cathedral (67)
Sableye, Spiritomb, Dusknoir, Cofagrigus, Gengar, Chandelure "Vigil".
- **Plan.** Ghost ×1.5, Will-O-Wisp never misses, Fire moves burn as ghostfire (also Ghost), Mystical Fire ×1.3.
- **Seed.** Cofagrigus: **Magical Seed** (+1 Sp. Def on top of Mummy's +1).
- **Stage play / answer to the flip.** Three holy moves (Dazzling Gleam is one) consecrate the nave and weaken
  Ghost. Two Ghost rites by a Ghost type bring the spirits back: Spiritomb's Curse, Gengar's Destiny Bond.
- **Counterplay.** Dark types (Cofagrigus's Body Press and Dusknoir's Brick Break are waiting), Normal moves (the
  veil is thin: Normal hits Ghost here), or consecrate and keep her rite users down.
- She has no route to the Inverse Field (nobody on the team learns Shadow Force or Boomburst).

### Gym 15: Magnus — Factory (71)
Ferrothorn, Excadrill, Kingambit, Klinklang "Mark One", Scizor, Metagross "Engine".
- **Plan.** Steel ×1.5, Gear Grind ×2, Gyro Ball ×1.5, hammers ×1.3 (Engine's Hammer Arm).
- **Seed.** Mark One: **Synthetic Seed** (+1 Attack, +1 Speed), then Shift Gear (+1 Defense as well here).
- **Transition play / answer to the flip.** Three quakes or one explosion wreck the Factory into a Scrapyard, and
  Earthquake is the natural anti-Steel move. Mark One's Magnet Rise dodges the quakes (+1 Speed here) and, with
  Wild Charge, restarts the line: three charging moves, transform strike on the third if it is Wild Charge.
- **Counterplay.** Fire, Fighting, and wrecking the place: in the Scrapyard Shift Gear fails and Water is super
  effective on Steel.
- Nobody on his team quakes or explodes any more (Excadrill and Engine did), and Excadrill is Mold Breaker: Sand
  Rush could never work indoors.

### Gym 16: Nyx — New Moon (75, double in the story)
Grimmsnarl, Weavile, Malamar, Umbreon, Zoroark "Nobody" (disguised as the last slot), Honchkrow.
- **Plan.** Dark ×1.5; everything that is not Dark or Ghost aims at ×0.9.
- **Stage play.** Weavile's **Magical Seed** gives +1 accuracy and Focus Energy and pulls the field into the Abyss
  as it enters (shadow moves ×1.4, the player's accuracy ×0.8); Night Slash then always crits. Three Night Dazes do
  the same and never miss.
- **Answer to the flip.** Three light moves light the lamps (Dark ×1.2). Any three of her Dark attacks snuff them;
  until then Nobody's Flamethrower is ×1.2 by lamplight and Sludge Bomb is for the Fairy that lit them.
- **Counterplay.** Fairy, Fighting, Bug; Keen Eye, Compound Eyes and No Guard see in the dark.
- Umbreon uses Wish: Moonlight fails under a new moon.

### Gym 17: Celestine — Fairy Tale (80, double in the story)
Alcremie, Mimikyu, Azumarill, Tinkaton, Togekiss "Vigilant", **Sylveon "Vow"** (ace, per the story bible).
- **Plan.** Fairy ×1.5; Vow's Draining Kiss is ×2 here; Tinkaton is the knight (Steel ×1.3).
- **Seed.** Alcremie: **Magical Seed** (+1 Sp. Atk, +1 Sp. Def), plus Decorate for the double.
- **Transition play / answer to the flip.** Three Dragon attacks rouse the Dragon's Den. Any three of her Fairy
  attacks tame it back with a transform strike.
- **Steel is the real threat**, and Fire does not answer it here (dragonfire: Fire also hits as Dragon, which Steel
  resists). The answers are Azumarill's Superpower and Togekiss's Aura Sphere.
- **Counterplay.** Steel and Poison; lead Poison, since Tinkaton walls it but cannot hurt it much.
- Hard's two-bar boss rule (last Pokémon of the team) now lands on Sylveon.

### Gym 18: Sigrun — Dragon's Den (85)
Flygon, Kommo-o, Dragonite, Haxorus, Dragapult, Salamence "Dreamwing".
- **Plan.** Dragon ×1.5 and her Dragons take ×0.83. Four Dragon attacks awaken the den (Dragon ×2). Dragon Dance
  gives +2 Speed here.
- **Field picks.** Dragonite's Multiscale makes Dragon's weaknesses neutral in the Den (it replaces Goodra).
  Dreamwing holds an **Amulet Coin**: the hoard protects its own, so it cannot be statused.
- **Transition play / answer to the flip.** Three Water or Ice attacks cool the Den to a Cave; Dragapult's
  **Draco Meteor** reopens it in one move with a transform strike, Kommo-o's Clanging Scales in two. Three Fairy
  attacks make it a Fairy Tale; three of her Dragon attacks take it back (seen in the harness run).
- **Counterplay.** Fairy with Steel backup; Ice is ×0.7 here. Kommo-o's Flash Cannon and Haxorus's Poison Jab are
  her Fairy answers.
