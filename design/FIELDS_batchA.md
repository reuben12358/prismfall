## Colosseum — Gym 8 (Fighting), Ironreach (Nadir)
*The crowd roars as the fighters enter the pit!*
- ↑ Fighting ×1.2. All punching moves ×1.2 (elemental punches too). Kicks (Double Kick, Jump Kick, High Jump Kick, Low Kick, Rolling Kick, Mega Kick, Triple Kick, Triple Axel, Blaze Kick, Trop Kick, Thunderous Kick, Axe Kick, Low Sweep, Pyro Ball) ×1.2.
- ↑ Throws into the cage (Circle Throw, Storm Throw, Vital Throw, Dragon Tail, Submission) ×1.5. Focus Punch ×1.5. Comebacks (Revenge, Payback, Retaliate, Avalanche, Lash Out) ×1.3.
- **The crowd**: knocking out a foe gives the attacker +1 Attack (physical finisher) or +1 Sp. Atk (special finisher). **Underdog**: a Pokémon at 1/3 HP or less deals ×1.2 damage.
- Focus Blast ×1.2 accuracy. Focus Energy also gives +1 Attack. Inner Focus / Steadfast: +1 critical-hit stage. Sand Attack drops accuracy one extra stage (sand floor).
- ✕ Double Team, Minimize (nowhere to hide). ✕ Roar, Whirlwind (no one leaves the pit).
- Telluric Seed: +1 Attack and Focus Energy.
- Nature Power: Brick Break · Camouflage: Fighting.

## Flower Garden — Gym 9 (Grass), Verdance (Elowen)
*Tiny buds dot the garden beds.*
The garden has **5 growth stages** (the arena's starting stage depends on the gym puzzle).
- **Grows +1 stage**: Growth, Rototiller, Flower Shield, Ingrain, Water Sport, Aromatherapy, Floral Healing, Life Dew, Grass Pledge, Water Pledge; Sun or Rain starting; Grassy Terrain starting; every 2 damaging Grass moves.
- **Shrinks −1 stage**: any damaging Fire move (stages 1–4); non-Grass slicing moves (Slash, X-Scissor, Night Slash, Air Slash…); Hail starting.
- ⟳ At stage 5, 2 damaging Fire moves burn it down → **Ashen Waste**.
- Grassy Terrain lasts 3 turns longer at every stage.

| Stage | Grass | Bug | Fire | Petal moves* | Extras |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Buds | ×1.1 | — | — | — | |
| 2 Sprouts | ×1.2 | ×1.1 | — | ×1.2 | |
| 3 Blooms | ×1.3 | ×1.2 | ×1.1 | ×1.3 | Bloom effects (below); grounded Grass types heal 1/16 per turn |
| 4 Full flower | ×1.5 | ×1.3 | ×1.3 | ×1.5 | + Bug types heal 1/16 per turn; Chlorophyll ×1.5 Speed without sun |
| 5 Overgrown | ×2.0 | ×1.5 | ×1.5 | ×1.5 | as stage 4; Fire now burns the garden down |

\*Petal Dance, Petal Blizzard, Flower Trick, Pollen Puff, Fleur Cannon.
- **Bloom effects (stage 3+)**: Overgrow always active. Sweet Scent also lowers Defense and Sp. Def. Growth gives an extra +1 Attack / +1 Sp. Atk. Grass Pelt: ×1.5 Defense.
- Telluric Seed: +1 Sp. Def and Ingrain, and the garden grows one stage.
- Nature Power: Magical Leaf (1–2), Petal Blizzard (3–4), Petal Dance (5) · Camouflage: Grass.
- Counterplay: Bug and Fire grow with the garden too. Cut it back with blades or fire before Juno's team snowballs, or burn the overgrowth down.

## Frozen Lake — Gym 10 (Ice), Rimeholt (Isenmar)
*The battle is taking place on a frozen lake!*
- ↑ Ice ×1.5. Skating: Rapid Spin, Rollout, Ice Ball ×1.5; Triple Axel, Ice Spinner, Spin Out ×1.3.
- **Sliding**: grounded Pokémon using Quick Attack, Extreme Speed, Aqua Jet, Accelerock, Feint, Mach Punch, Bullet Punch, Jet Punch, First Impression or Shadow Sneak gain +1 Speed.
- Icy Wind / Glaciate drop Speed one extra stage. Slush Rush ×1.5 Speed without hail; Ice Body heals 1/16 per turn without hail.
- ✕ Dig (the ice is too thick). Hail lasts 3 turns longer. Grassy Terrain can't form.
- ⟳ **Cracking the ice**: 3 hits from any mix of damaging Fire moves, Earthquake, Magnitude, Bulldoze, Fissure, Stomping Tantrum, High Horsepower, Headlong Rush, Heavy Slam or Body Press → **Water Surface**. Sun starting → **Water Surface** (melted).
- Elemental Seed: +1 Defense, +1 Speed.
- Nature Power: Ice Beam · Camouflage: Ice.

## Stormfront — Gym 11 (Flying), Skyreach (Zenith)
*Gale-force winds howl across the airship deck!*

- Sun can't form (the storm swallows it). Electric Terrain lasts 3 turns longer.
- Elemental Seed: +1 Speed and Charge.
- Nature Power: Hurricane · Camouflage: Flying.

## Crystal Cavern — Gym 13 (Rock), Cragholm (Ossaria)
*Prismatic crystals glitter all around!*
- ↑ Rock ×1.3. Damaging Sound moves ×1.2 (the crystals ring). Ancient Power's stat boost is twice as likely.
- **Prism glow**: the crystals glow one colour, starting at random and cycling each turn: red (Fire) → blue (Water) → green (Grass) → amethyst (Psychic). Damaging Rock moves **also hit as the glow's type**, and moves **of** the glow's type are ×1.2.
- ↑ Light moves ×1.5: Aurora Beam, Signal Beam, Flash Cannon, Luster Purge, Mist Ball, Dazzling Gleam, Mirror Shot, Doom Desire, Moongeist Beam, Photon Geyser, Prismatic Laser, Power Gem, Diamond Storm, Meteor Beam, Lumina Crash, Charge Beam, Techno Blast.
- Flash drops accuracy one extra stage. Reflect, Light Screen and Aurora Veil last 2 turns longer.
- ⟳ Dark Pulse, Night Daze, Dark Void or Fiery Wrath, or a Sandstorm starting → **Dark Crystal Cavern**. Explosion / Self-Destruct / Misty Explosion, or 3 uses of Earthquake / Magnitude / Bulldoze / Fissure → **Cave** (crystals shattered).
- Telluric Seed: +1 Defense, +1 Sp. Def.
- Nature Power: Power Gem · Camouflage: Rock.

## Dark Crystal Cavern
*Darkness fills the cavern. The crystals have gone black...*
- ↑ Dark ×1.5, Ghost ×1.3, Rock ×1.2 (obsidian). Stone Edge, Stone Axe ×1.2.
- Light moves (the Crystal Cavern list) ×1.2 — they pierce the dark.
- Damaging moves from Pokémon that aren't Dark, Ghost or Rock have ×0.9 accuracy (Keen Eye, Illuminate, Mind's Eye, No Guard ignore this). Illuminate: +1 accuracy on entry.
- Dark and Ghost contact moves: +1 critical-hit stage. Flash drops accuracy one extra stage.
- ⟳ 2 uses of Flash, Dazzling Gleam, Flash Cannon, Power Gem, Diamond Storm, Luster Purge, Prismatic Laser, Photon Geyser, Lumina Crash, Aurora Beam, Mirror Shot, Tail Glow, Light of Ruin or Meteor Beam, or Sun starting → **Crystal Cavern** (relit). Explosions, or 3 quakes → **Cave**.
- Magical Seed: +1 Attack, +1 Sp. Atk.
- Nature Power: Dark Pulse · Camouflage: Dark.
