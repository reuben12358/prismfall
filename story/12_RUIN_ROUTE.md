# Story Bible 12: The Ruin Route — "Every Type and None" (R1–R7)

> Mechanics, unlock and endings are specified in `10_CHOICES_ENDINGS.md` §5 and NARRATIVE_KEYS §11. This file is the chapter outline at the same depth as the main acts. It **replaces the rest of Ch15 through Ch21** on a save with a full Ledger where the PC answers *yes* to **Look back?** (`:ch15_look_back`) after the Giratina boss.
> **Tone:** quiet, cold and lucid. The PC isn't a villain. They have seen every way this world ends and have stopped asking it to be saved. Everyone who loved them on the main route still does, which is the horror of it.
> **Revisions 4–6 applied:** no sister-game cameos in the Festival (Nova is an R5 sidequest); every opponent is met earlier on this route or the main one; the Festival is the ceremony of the Blessing; the war still happens.

**What this route reveals** (never stated on the main route): the Ledger is real; Antumbra is an *unfinished Judgment*; the Hymn has a third verse, "the verse of none"; "I learned that here" means Arceus stopped one heartbeat short of unmaking Aurelia; and the Gilded King is still alive behind the Glass.

## R1 — "Twenty-Three Graves" (the Reverse Depths)
- **Location:** Giratina's Reverse Depths (806, `:INVERSE`), then a new floor beneath it.
- **Beats:**
  1. The PC steps through the black glass. Juno's reflection steps aside, and it isn't Juno.
  2. The floor is a field of **twenty-three graves**, each carved with one of the PC's epitaphs from the Ledger. "You have no Imprint in Velmora. In here, you have twenty-three."
  3. **The Reflection** stands up from the last grave: the PC's own sprite, mirrored, the one who remembers dying every time. It speaks in the PC's authored lines, reversed ("Yet not... too scared I'm... me show").
  4. **Mirror battle: the Reflection.** The PC's own party at their levels, on `:INVERSE`.
  5. **Giratina (Origin) tests the Renegade**: a multi-bar trial, not a hunt. It ends when the PC stops attacking for one turn.
  6. Giratina gives the PC **the none**. Resonance locks at Ruin (≤ −6). The Ledger becomes a Key Item, **the Black Case**, and the PC's badge case turns black to match.
- **New traversal: Mirrorwalk.** Marked mirrors in Zenith, Nadir, Elowen and Ashkaar open onto Reverse versions of those maps.
- **Archseer Dask** is waiting at the Depths' mouth (third meeting): "Welcome, behind. You looked. I told you never to look. I'm glad one of us disobeys."
- **Cliffhanger:** the Reflection falls into step behind the PC like a second shadow. Only Pokémon and Celestine can see it.

## R2 — "Built to Break" (Cogsworth, Gym 15)
- **Premise:** the PC now *wants* the Engine finished. It is the only machine that can hold all eighteen Plates still long enough to unmake them.
- **Beats:**
  1. Cogsworth as in Ch16, seen colder: the PC walks past Rook's canteen table. If the player sits anyway, Rook: "You look like me after the Lowlight."
  2. **Zekrom** must still be freed, so the core fails and Voss reroutes everything to the Temple-Arena. The boss plays as written, but when it's free it doesn't flee. It looks at the PC and *recoils*.
  3. `:ch16_magnus` reads differently. *Expose* ruins him so nobody can stop the build. *Conscience* persuades him to build it "to break right", and he understands exactly what he's being asked.
- **Gym 15 (Ruin):** Magnus on `:FACTORY`, which becomes `:SCRAPYARD` on turn one. "You came here to break it. So did I, once."
- **War beat:** the foundry is casting Prism lances for both armies. The PC can stop it or not, and the game no longer tells them which is kinder.
- **Cliffhanger:** the Sump Purge on the screens. The Reflection says its first forward sentence: "We died there once. It didn't help."

## R3 — "The Sun Court Below" (Underveil, Gym 16; Aurelia)
- **Beats:**
  1. Underveil after the Purge. **Nyx sides with the Renegade** without being asked: her house kept the drowned truth for three hundred years and she's tired of it. "You're going to end the secret-keeping business. I should be upset."
  2. **Gym 16 (Ruin):** the illusion double battle, with `:NEW_MOON` running Inverse matchups.
  3. Aurelia by Mirrorwalk: the Sun Court's mirror-wall is a window. Behind the glass the court stands frozen at the instant of the Calling, and **Theodric's reflection** is in it mid-command, the reflected Chain in his hands. His eyes move. (First meeting with the route's hijacker.)
  4. **Voss walks in**, sees him, and falls to his knees: "He's alive. Behind the glass he's still alive." He leaves with the cradle, serving a dead king now. The PC watches the most reasonable man in Velmora become a zealot in ten seconds.
- **Game over hook:** `GO_THE_THRONE` is already in the Ledger, so the throne's armrests are closed fists now. Sitting does nothing. The Reflection: "We've done that one."
- **Cliffhanger:** Theodric's reflection mouths one word at the PC through the glass: *"Finish."*

## R4 — "Midnight in Gloaming" (Gym 17; Pecharunt; K7)
- **Beats:**
  1. Pecharunt in the Lightless Deep, as written. The thralls wake and thank the PC, and it lands wrong.
  2. **Celestine** tells the PC the **verse of none**, which Maren carved on the Reverse side of the Concordance: *"And if the walker comes with no color in their heart, the god will come home to finish what it began."* "I cannot lie, so I stopped singing it."
  3. She refuses the badge, and fights for it anyway. **Gym 17 (Ruin):** `:FAIRY_TALE` turns `:INVERSE` for her last Pokémon ("Once upon a time, which was the only time").
  4. The dusk does **not** lift. Ogerpon, if it walked with the PC, leaves at the Verge and goes back to the Bride Tree.
  5. **K7** plays as written, but Kestrel knows: "You're going to *erase* her? Everything she paid for?" `:ch18_kestrel_ring` is still offered. Giving it is the last warm thing on the route.
- **Cliffhanger:** `GO_STAY_OUT`'s prompt appears and can't be answered *yes*. The cursor won't move there.

## R5 — "The Last Gate" (Wyrmrest, Gym 18; the Glass Dunes; Nova)
- **Beats:**
  1. **The oath** can only be true one way: "I'm going to end it. All of it." Reshiram accepts the truth and flies to the front without a fight.
  2. **Gym 18 (Ruin):** Sigrun on `:DRAGONS_DEN`, no Reshiram, every Pokémon a step faster. "Dragons don't let anything through they haven't weighed. I've weighed you."
  3. **The Battle of the Glass Dunes** (14_WAR_AND_BLESSING). The PC can't *stand between*: the option reads "End it." The shard doesn't calm the beam clash. It **eats** it, and both legendaries' beams go out like candles. WAR-1 plays with shots 10–12 replaced: the clash-ball turns black and vanishes, the flood stops dead and sinks into the sand, Kyogre and Reshiram both flee the PC, and both armies stop fighting out of fear. The truce is called anyway. Nobody thanks the PC.
  4. The Dominar can't be swayed, and neither ruler will ride with the Renegade.
- **Sidequest: Nova** (CAMEOS.md §9), on the Mirrorwalk dream-road out of Gloaming. She offers a gentler unmaking: everyone asleep and happy forever. Accepting is **`GO_R_SWEET_DREAMS`**. Refusing is a fight, and the **Crescent Charm**.
- **Cliffhanger:** on the Sovereign's Ascent the Wardguard bars the road. Dask's Synod opens a mirror in the cliff instead.

## R6 — "The Unfestival" (Festival week)
- **Beats:**
  1. The Accord bars "the Renegade" from the Blessing. No kingdom offers its colors. **Dask** enters the PC as Antumbra's second delegate, "Unsworn, behind", and **Dorian**, who has never once let the bowl ruin a story, seeds them himself.
  2. **Opening ceremony:** the Plates dim as the PC passes each pylon. The vision of Arceus is the same eye, but it speaks differently: *"Little light. You have carried me far. Why are you so cold?"*
  3. **R16: Juno.** "You'll make sure Verdance never gets an autumn." She fights to stop the PC, and cries the whole match.
  4. **QF: Archseer Dask** (met Ch13, Ch15, R1). He fights to *measure* the PC, on `:INVERSE`: "Behind, welcome. Welcome, behind. The Synod must know what it is letting through."
  5. **SF: Kestrel.** He tears the Gauntlet off to fight the PC as himself. If he has the ring: "She was nearest. I'm nearest now. Stop."
  6. **Night of Masques:** the actor playing the Gilded King freezes mid-line, because the mirror behind the stage is showing the real one.
  7. **Valen's audience** is three lines long. Valen: "I know what you are now. I'll hold them quiet one more time. I'll die doing it. That's still better than you."
  8. **Final: Valen, the Last Tuning.** He fights with the Crown on and the Plates lit behind him.
- **No exhibitions** except the Eevee Octave (its gauntlet still needs all eight singers helped). Vesper hosts it without once looking at the PC, but Understudy's siblings do.
- **Cliffhanger:** the Blessing. Nobody will raise the Crown. The PC picks it up off the arena floor.

## R7 — "Every Type and None" (the finale)
- **Beats:**
  1. The Engine wakes, but the hand that reaches up through the arena's crystal walls isn't only Voss's. **Theodric's reflection** steps through wearing the Chain, and the Aureate court is visible behind him in every reflective surface in Crownhaven.
  2. Voss kneels. Theodric invites the PC to kneel with him: **`GO_R_KNEEL`**.
  3. Theodric casts Voss aside into the Chain. **CHOICE `:ruin_voss`:** *spare* him (the PC pulls him out; he lives, hollow) or *abandon* him.
  4. **Hijack: Theodric, the Gilded Reflection.** His team is the Gilders' court: Golurk, Aegislash, Gholdengo-gold reflections of Wardens' aces, on `:PRISM_ARENA` running Inverse.
  5. **Arceus descends**, not dragged but *coming*, to finish what it began in AF 0. Valen holds the Ring one last time, to keep the crowd alive while god and Renegade fight. No Warden releases anything; all eighteen locks hold.
  6. **True Finale: Arceus (Judgment)**, seven bars, with **Giratina at the PC's side**. The last bar is on `:INVERSE` as Giratina turns the arena inside out.
  7. **The Unmaking needs a vessel.** The *none* unmakes the Chain, the Plates, Judgment and finally the Glass, and whoever carries it goes with the Glass. The Reflection offers: "I've done this twenty-three times. Let me do it once on purpose."
  8. **CHOICE `:ruin_walk`:** who walks out of the Glass.
- **Endings** (10_CHOICES_ENDINGS §5.4):
  - **`:me` → "The Unmaking".** The Reflection is unmade with the Glass. Every Imprint ends at once. All the Fixed wake, even the long-Fixed, some of them as strangers. Verdance gets its autumn, and Juno won't look at the PC while it falls. Arceus leaves as a light with no color and never returns. The PC is the only person who remembers the old world and twenty-three ways it ended.
  - **`:reflection` → "The One Who Came Back"** (secret). The PC is unmade, and the Reflection walks out wearing their face. The epilogue is the same world seen slightly wrong: a signature mirrored once and corrected, and Juno saying "You never used to hum." Last image: Hesper's cracked mirror, with nothing behind it.
- **Post-game:** Mirrorwalk maps stay open as ordinary places, Giratina is catchable, there is no Arceus, and the Blessing is never held again.
