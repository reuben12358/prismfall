## PART A — ARCEUS'S BLESSING (the Festival reframed)

### What everyone believes
The Festival of Champions is the one sacred ceremony all seven kingdoms share. Every ten years each kingdom carries its Plates to Crownhaven and sends **two delegates** to fight before the Plates. The last one standing receives **Arceus's Blessing**:
1. **The Light.** The Crown is set on the victor inside the Concordance Ring, and the light of all eighteen Plates passes through them. The Choir teaches that this is Arceus touching one mortal.
2. **The Seat.** The Blessed holds the Sovereign Seat for the decade.
3. **The Boon.** The Blessed may speak **one decree that every crown must honor until the next Festival**: a border, a peace, a tax, a pardon. This is why kings care, and why they send their best. Maren's first Boon founded the Wardguard. Valen's three Boons: the Plate-tax ban (AF 270), the Marrowgate silence (AF 280, sealed), the Morwen verdict (AF 290, sealed).

### What is true
- **The Light is the Tuning.** "The Plates' light passing through the Blessed" is the bearer's body hushing the Plates. The Blessing shortens every life it touches. Nothing in 01 §5 changes except the name the public uses.
- **The Boon is real law** and was Maren's own invention: a reward large enough that the strongest person in the realm would always volunteer for a thing that kills them.
- **Valen** has been Blessed three times. He intends the PC to be the fourth name on the list and the last.
- **Voss** needs the Blessing moment itself: eighteen Plates converged, the Crown raised, a bearer in the Ring. The Arbiter Engine fires *during the Light*. He also wants Zenith's delegates to win the Boon as a fallback ("Mandate in perpetuity").
- **The Hymn of Return** read correctly: the last Blessed gives the Light *back*.

### Retcons (small)
- "Sovereign" = "the Blessed". The Coronation in Ch21 is "the Blessing". Arceus's speech gains one line: *"They call this my blessing. I have never once been asked."*
- The Good ending adds a Boon beat: with the Plates gone, the PC still gets one decree. **CHOICE `:ch21_boon` *(proposed)*:** end the war's reparations / seat Nadir on every council / dissolve the Seat itself.
- Entrants are **delegations**, not a free draw (below). The bowl still decides pairings.

## PART B — THE ASHKAAR–ISENMAR WAR

### Why they fight (and why it isn't a detour)
- **Isenmar's heat is Ashkaar's fire.** Meridian's heat-towers in Rimeholt are fed by Prism Cells charged at the **Cindervault geothermal tap**. Ashkaar's Flame Imprint is being drained to keep Isenmar's children warm. Meridian sold it to both crowns as two unrelated contracts.
- **Old grievance:** the River Ebb rises in Ashkaar's highlands as meltwater. Each kingdom claims the headwaters. Isenmar's ice-fleet and Ashkaar's riders have skirmished there for a century.
- **Voss wants the war.** Two opposed Imprints colliding at scale is the best calibration data the Engine can get short of the Festival. Meridian arms both sides with Prism weapons and logs every clash. The war also keeps two crowns too busy to vote against the Mandate.
- **The Bleed:** every battlefield where Fire and Ice Imprints meet becomes a Bleed zone of steam-glass. The front line is a Bleed line, and only the PC can cross it.
- **The legendaries:** Kyogre (woken by the Thaw) and Reshiram (the Light Stone) become the kingdoms' banners. The climax is their clash.

### The Battle of the Shattered Ice (Ch19)
**Setup.** The front is on the north-east coast (WORLD.md §8–9): Isenmar's fortress-port **Skaldfast** is besieged from the **Ashkaar Siege Camp**, the **Cinder Cape Warfort** guards Ashkaar's shore, the **Rime Narrows** between them are choked with the ice-fleet, and the Bleed line of steam-glass where the Imprints meet is **the Steamline**. Sigrun's trial and oath (08, Ch19) happen first, at Wyrmrest. Then word comes that Kyogre has risen from the Ice Vault and is walking the Narrows like a tide, and that her father has carried the Light Stone to the front. Sigrun flies the PC there overnight. The climax is fought on **the Shattered Ice**, the broken sea between the two hosts.

**The six war choices** (keys and effects: NARRATIVE_KEYS §4.3; this is their staging):
1. **`:war_warning`** (Ch11, before the Thaw). Climbing **Frostfang Pass**, the PC sees the Dominar's legions massing before anyone else knows. Warn Isenmar, warn Ashkaar that they've been seen, warn both through Valen, or stay silent.
2. **`:war_convoy`** (Ch14). Marisol's blockade-runners ask the Unimprinted to walk a convoy through the Steamline into besieged **Skaldfast**: *relief* (food), *arms* (harpoon-guns), or *refuse*.
3. **`:war_prisoner`** (Ch15–16, by Gale Post and a short trip to the Siege Camp). Ashkaar holds **Anja**; the Thane holds the Dominar's nephew. *Free* her by walking her out through the Bleed line, broker an *exchange*, or *leave* it. This is Anja's extra pre-Festival scene: in the cage she says, "Tell my brother I'd have signed for the heaters again."
4. **`:war_legend`** (Ch17–18). Each side begs the PC to bring its legend to the front: *calm Kyogre* in the Ice Vault, *wake Reshiram* early with the oath, or *neither*.
5. **`:war_climax`** (Ch19, MAJOR). On the Shattered Ice: *stand between*, *back Ashkaar* or *back Isenmar*.
6. **`:war_terms`** (Ch19 aftermath, only if one side won). At the armistice table in Skaldfast's great hall the PC argues for *mercy*, *reparations* or *occupation*.

**The four outcomes:**
- **`WAR_STOPPED`** (stood between, and both armies have seen enough of the PC to trust them: War Trust ≥ 4). Both lines hold fire. The beams meet over the PC and part.
- **`WAR_BOTH_BROKEN`** (stood between without that trust). Nobody listens. The clash detonates and breaks both armies. Both kingdoms forfeit a Festival seat.
- **`WAR_ASHKAAR_WON`** / **`WAR_ISENMAR_WON`.** The loser forfeits a seat. *Occupation* terms poison the Good ending: Arceus won't bless a land with a boot on its neck.

**The battle.** Playable set pieces around the cutscene: a gauntlet through the camp of the side you didn't choose (or both, if you stand between), a partner battle with Sigrun or Marisol, then the boss fight **as written for Reshiram** in 08, reframed: the PC fights whichever legendary is about to fire on the other army (Reshiram by default; **Kyogre** if backing Ashkaar). If `:war_legend` kept both legends away, the clash is between the Wardens' aces instead: Sigrun's Salamence against Anja's Mamoswine or Marisol's Gyarados.

**The climax** is the pixel-animated cutscene **WAR-1 "The Beam Clash on the Shattered Ice"** (`13_CUTSCENES.md`): Reshiram's Blue Flare meets Kyogre's Origin Pulse over the Shattered Ice. Where they meet, the air turns to steam-glass and a Bleed dome swells over both armies. The PC's shard (and the two Wardens who love each side) ends it.

**Aftermath.**
- Meridian observers on the ridge pack up their instruments before the steam clears. Larch, by radio: "That wasn't a battle to them. That was a *reading*." Field Notes logs the Engine's calibration jumping.
- Sigrun and Anja (or Marisol) stand on the glass between the armies and call the truce: the war will be settled by the Boon. The rulers agree because their legendaries have both left: Reshiram to Sigrun, Kyogre back to the sea.

### What the war changes later
- **Consent:** side-taking adds refusals (Brann or Anja/Marisol). Standing between adds none.
- **Hijack montage:** if the truce held without a victor, Ashkaar's riders *and* Isenmar's ice-fleet hold the sky and the lake together.
- **Endings:** Homecoming's Boon can end reparations. The Gilded Chain: Meridian annexes both exhausted kingdoms. Hollow Spring: the war restarts with no Plates and no fire.
