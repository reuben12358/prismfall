# Story Bible 15: Comedy (SPINE Revision 7 — "we need funny moments!")

> The game gets darker every act. That is exactly why it needs people being ridiculous at each other. This file is the humor guide, the recurring dynamics, the running gags, one scripted funny scene per chapter (Ch0–Ch21), optional bits per kingdom, and the portrait-art list.
> **Script format:** `[portrait: NAME, expression]` then the line. Expressions: neutral, happy, smug, angry, crying, shocked, deadpan, panic, sad, blush. Emotes in *(italics)*: sweat drop, shock lines, anger vein, "..." bubble, music note, heart, question mark.
> The PC's replies are choice prompts shown as `> option`. The PC has no portrait.

---

## 1. Humor guide

- **Where the comedy comes from:** people who care about each other, losing patience. The PC is a fifteen-year-old who walks into Bleed zones, vaults and wars as a habit. Every adult who loves them reacts the way that Rejuvenation portrait does: crying *and* yelling.
- **Character-based, never quippy.** A line is funny because of who says it. If the line could be handed to anyone, cut it.
- **The silent PC is a comedy engine.** Characters argue with the PC's silence, answer questions nobody asked, and read whole speeches into a "...". The three choice options are often two sensible replies and one that is clearly a bad idea, and the bad idea gets the best reaction.
- **Absurdity lives in optional content:** signs, NPCs, sidequests, shop names. The main path stays grounded.
- **Never undercut the big beats.** No jokes inside: the Lowlight Shatter aftermath, Rowan's unmasking, the Thaw, the Fault choice, the Cathedral echo, Interlude IV, Valen's audience, the finale from the hijack onward. A joke may come *before* a blow (it makes the blow land) or a full scene *after* (it lets the player breathe). Never during.
- **Comedy tracks the tone curve.** Act I: broad and warm. Act III onward: drier, shorter, gallows. By Act V a single dry line from Corvin is the whole joke.
- **Jokes can carry plot.** Several running gags below are foreshadowing wearing a silly hat.

## 2. Comic dynamics

### A. "The Lecture" — Larch (exasperated) and the PC (chaos, silently)
Larch has kept this child alive by post for six months and is discovering they treat every Bleed Veil like a doorway. She starts each scolding as a scientist and ends it as a parent. She cries when she's angriest and denies it ("It's the tea. The tea is very strong."). Brann and Corvin inherit the role when she's absent, in their own registers: Brann booms and then hugs; Corvin lectures in the form of a medical bill.

### B. "The Committee" — Juno (earnest chaos), Kestrel (furious straight man), the PC (tie-breaker)
Juno narrates plant facts at dramatic moments and adopts things. Kestrel has rehearsed being cool and is ambushed by Juno's kindness every time. Their bickering is the party banter. Kestrel insists he isn't part of any group; the group has a name (Juno named it; see the gag).

### C. "The Wardens' Group Chat" — Odessa (chaos), Ilsa (deadpan), Dorian (ham), Bastian (selectively deaf)
From Act III the Wardens share Gale Post relay messages the PC can read at any Pokémon Center ("Warden Circular"). Odessa posts nonsense. Dorian replies in stage directions. Ilsa answers thoughts nobody typed. Bastian replies to the wrong message a week late. Anja's only contribution, every time: "Toes."

## 3. Running gags (with payoffs)

| 4 | **Rook's chat** | Rook relays what his stream chat says. Chat is always wrong about battles and always right about feelings. | **Hijack:** Rook cuts the grid; his stream dies. First line offline: "Chat? ...Oh. It's just me." Then, quieter: "Okay. Still charged." |
| 5 | **Bastian's deafness** | He can't hear questions about fugitives, taxes or bedtime. | 6 | **Understudy never gets the part** | The gift Eevee is cast in every Saltmere play and cut before curtain (as Tree #3, as "a bush", as the understudy to a prop). | 7 | **Corvin's bills** | After every reckless thing the PC does in his sight, he hands them an itemized invoice ("Item: one (1) heart attack, mine. Item: gauze."). The total is always ₽0. | **Epilogue:** the last invoice arrives by post. "Item: one (1) world, saved. Paid in full." |
| 8 | **The mirror that waves late** (dark) | From Ch12 the PC's reflection lags. Optional mirror interactions are jokes: it's caught yawning, it fixes its hair, it mimes "behind you" when nothing is there. Juno finds it hilarious. | **Ruin Route, R1:** the Reflection: "You laughed at me in Skyreach. I was trying to *warn* you." No joke after that is safe in a mirror. |
