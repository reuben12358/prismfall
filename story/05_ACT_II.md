# Story Bible 05: Act II — Imprints (Ch6–9, Gyms 5–8)

> **Tone:** Reborn-grey. The cost of Velmora's miracles becomes visible: company towns, Fixing wards, a street drug, an act of terror, and an unmasking that hurts someone you like. Humor stays, but it's gallows humor now.
> Conventions as in 04 (CHOICE / GO / map names per WORLD.md).
> **Revision 6:** this act carries beats of the Ashkaar–Isenmar War and the Blessing. See `14_WAR_AND_BLESSING.md` (timeline table) for the inserts per chapter; that file wins on conflicts.


**Act-opening beat (start of segment 4):** the PC leaves Lumen City through the **Lowlight (Shattered)** (608, `:BLEED`). A shrine of soda cans and candles has grown around Dex's glass figure. If the PC stands with him, the shard *hums* and the glass warms slightly under their hand. (Harmony seed for the Good ending's waking.) Mandate checkpoints with Directorate officers in white-and-gold now stand at every lift down to Nadir.

## CHAPTER 6 — "What the Sand Keeps" (R9 → Static Tunnels → R10 → Duneholt, Gym 5; Ossuary of Dunes; K3)

**Location:** R9, **Static Tunnels** (Flash), R10 (`:DESERT`), **Duneholt** (Ossaria), R11, **Ossuary of Dunes** (Unown ruins, Strength), **Sinkfield** (628).

**Borderland:** Zenith's plateau gives way to **Ossaria, the Bone Sands**. Fossil ribs as big as houses poke from the dunes. Caravan-houses pitch tents in the lee of them. NPCs quote prices in "bones".

**Arrival.** Duneholt is sandstone terraces around a great **sinking well**. The aquifer the Earth Plate holds in the stone is collapsing under **Meridian's deep drills**, and every week the waterline drops another rung. The Prince-Elector's men post notices: *"By license of His Excellency Idris Kassan, the Concord deep-works are a protected enterprise."* Caravan-mothers curse at them.
- **Sahar's Dig Lab:** fossil revival opens here. Ossaria is fossil country, so a wide selection is available, and Sahar is delighted by anyone who brings her bones.
- Under the town, the ground is **warm**. Children say "the sleeping red giant" is turning over in its bed. (A Groudon seed.)

**Conflict.**
- A drill-site explodes. The **Hollow** freed Pokémon that Meridian had been using as living drill-cores (Excadrill and Drilbur fitted with Lattice collars). The Directorate arrives with the Mandate's new powers and starts arresting caravan kids.
- **CHOICE `:ch6_drillsite`:**
  - Help the Hollow get the freed Pokémon into the dunes: Hollow Standing up, Meridian Standing down. Directorate ambushes follow in Ch7.
  - Help the Directorate secure the site: Meridian Standing up, and you earn a "commendation" (a lightly sinister Key Item: a Mandate Pass that later opens checkpoints).
  - Get Sahar to invoke the Warden's right of sanctuary over the children: Accord Standing goes up and the Directorate backs off, furious. (Choosing the Directorate here is Sahar's Consent refusal in Act V.)

**Gym 5 (`:DESERT`).** Thematic note: *dig down, because what you need is buried.* Sahar is patient, dry and coughing: "The desert keeps everything. It just doesn't keep it where you left it." Her ace is **Claydol**. She gives Strength via her excavators.

**Aftermath and revelation: the Ossuary of Dunes (Unown ruins set piece).**
- The latest dune-shift has uncovered a doorway in a giant fossil skull on R11.
- Sahar goes pale. She has seen this before. She confesses: in **AF 281** she excavated these ruins, reported them to Valen, and resealed them on his order.
- Inside, **Unown** drift in the walls. The PC solves Unown-script puzzles (World/puzzle team) and reveals murals:
  1. A golden king, the Gilded King.
  2. Two titans, one red and one blue, tearing the land ("the Year of Two Weathers").
  3. A chain of eighteen golden locks around a radiant beast of eighteen lights.
  4. The beast **breaking apart**, with lights falling like rain and a city crumbling into the sea.
- Sahar takes a rubbing: the **Duneholt Rubbing** (Key Item). "I buried the truth once because I was told to. The sand dug it up. I'd rather not argue with sand twice."
- A boss-grade guardian (a giant Unown-swarm "glyph" formation) defends the deepest chamber. It's an optional multi-bar fight that rewards an Unown and the **Unown Report**.

**K3 (after Gym 5, at the Ossuary exit).** Kestrel has been following the Hollow's trail from Ebbmouth and arrived in time to see the murals over the PC's shoulder.
- After the battle he finally says it aloud: "Everyone keeps telling me to let it go. My mother had a seat, and a Plate, and a city that loved her. Then one night she had nothing. Let *what* go?" He lays out the rumor running all over Velmora: **the Mourner is Morwen Vane, alive.** "She wasn't killed by the Bleed. She was *made* to disappear."
- **CHOICE `:ch6_kestrel_rumor`:**
  - Encourage him to find out (Kestrel Bond up, and his obsession grows).
  - Warn him it might not be her (Bond down a little now, but it pays off at Ironreach, where he remembers you warned him).
  - Silence.

**Game over `GO_SINKFIELD` "Sinkfield".** **Sinkfield** (628) is a Bleed zone where the collapsing aquifer and the Earth Imprint have made the sand behave like water. Trigger: ignore Sahar's three warnings ("walk the bones, never the sand") and step off the fossil path to grab a glinting item. Foreshadowing: the Field Notes entry and a caravan-boy's lost sandal sinking slowly. *Epitaph: "The desert kept you. Somewhere else."*

**Cliffhanger:** A Gale Post courier finds the PC with a letter from Juno: *"Uncle Rowan's been 'away on Warden business' every time something happens. I'm being silly. Come to Mirefen? I'm helping Dr. Mott with his seed-medicine. Bring snacks."*

## CHAPTER 7 — "The Quiet Ward" (R12 → Mirefen, Gym 6; J2)

**Location:** R12, **Mirefen** (Nadir, `:CORROSIVE_MIRE`), the **Quiet Ward**, the Sump Barons' **Sweet Market**, R13, **Miasma Caverns**, **Weeping Fen** (648), R14 **Lake Sallow** (Surf after the badge).

**Arrival: first descent into Nadir.**
- The PC descends a rattling lift-cage from the plateau's edge. The sky disappears, and Zenith's underside is a ceiling of pipes dripping slag. The air tastes of pennies. Rebreathers are for sale on every corner.
- Hollow Ring graffiti is everywhere, often beside a painted **Ogre in the Mask** (Nadir's folk-saint).
- Mirefen is stilt-houses over a marsh that **glows blue at night**: Zenith's Lattice runoff mixing with the Toxic Plate's mire, the "poison-blue moon" of the rhyme.
- This is the best street food in Velmora (bog coffee, slag-fried noodles), and people are kinder here than topside.

**Conflict: Corvin and the Ward.**
- Warden **Corvin Mott** is gaunt, in a plague-doctor half-mask, and wickedly funny. He runs the Infirmary and its **Quiet Ward**: forty patients at Fixing stages 2–3, some humming, some half-glass, one a child whose hair has turned to salt.
- His apprentice **Wenlow** (17) is cheerful and clumsy, and introduces the PC to every patient by name. (Players should love Wenlow. Wenlow dies in Ch16.)
- **Sweetrot.** Pastel mochi-like sweets sold in the **Sweet Market** by smiling vendors of the Sump Barons (**Mother Gutter**'s people). Users go docile, loyal and blissful, and they keep coming back. Corvin's other ward is full of Sweetrot addicts. "It's not a drug. It's a *leash*." This is the Pecharunt seed: the sweets have a faint peach-and-poison smell, and a child's rhyme mentions "the peach that keeps you".
- **Game over `GO_SWEET_TOOTH` "Sweet Tooth".** Trigger: accept Sweetrot from a vendor *three times* across the chapter (each offer gets more insistent; Wenlow's warning comes after the first). *Epitaph: "You were so happy. You never wondered why again."*

**J2.**
- Juno is in the Infirmary helping Corvin brew seed-medicine from Verdance stock. She is luminous with purpose.
- Alphabetizing his ledgers, she finds an entry from the **night of the Lumen Shatter**: *"R.A. — 2nd-degree Bleed-burn, right palm and fingers. Lightning pattern. Discretion."* Corvin snatches it back too quickly.
- Juno laughs it off ("R.A., there's loads of R.A.s") and challenges the PC to a spar on the stilt-walk to shake off the feeling (**J2**). Mid-battle line: "He wouldn't. He *wouldn't*."
- **CHOICE `:ch7_ledger`:**
  - Say "Rowan Ashby" out loud: Juno's Bond drops sharply and she storms off. At Ironreach she is *less* shattered, because she already knew. This becomes a branch flavor in J3.
  - Reassure her: Juno's Bond goes up. The unmasking hits her full-force.
  - Ask Corvin directly, privately. He says only, "I'm a doctor. I treat hands. I don't ask whose."

**Hollow admin: Brother Wick** (`HOLLOW_WICK`).
- The PC tracks a Hollow medicine-runner to a drowned chapel on the edge of the **Weeping Fen**, a Bleed zone where rain falls upward and every puddle weeps.
- Brother Wick is old, half-blind and gentle, and he's here to bless dying Bereaved. He fights only because the PC forces it, or the PC can **talk him down** if Hollow Standing is high enough or they chose kindly in Ch5 (no battle).
- After the battle (or the talk) he explains the **Wound Doctrine**: "The Choir teaches that the Plates were a gift. Child, nobody bleeds for three hundred years from a *gift*." Then, more quietly, something new: *"The Mourner doesn't want to break them anymore. He wants to carry them home. But you can't carry what you can't hold."* He looks at the PC's chest and doesn't say why.

**Gym 6 (`:CORROSIVE_MIRE`), a double battle.** Thematic note: *poison is a dose; the right amount heals.* Corvin: "Don't worry. I'm a doctor. I know exactly how much this hurts." His ace is **Toxapex**. The Mirefen ferrymen give the PC Surf.

**Revelation.** That night Corvin drinks bog coffee with the PC on the Infirmary roof. "Do you know why nobody in my Ward is dead? They're not dying. They're *dreaming*. I can see their eyes move under the glass." A beat. "Somebody should find out what they're dreaming about." (This seeds the Good ending's waking and Venam's Act V testimony.)

**Optional thread: cameo sidequest "Sewer Royalty" (Venam).** See `design/CAMEOS.md`. A loud punk girl with a Seviper has fallen through a sewer mirror into Mirefen's runoff tunnels, and she is helping Wenlow drag Sweetrot addicts to the Infirmary. Completing it earns **Venam's Testimony**, a recording of her account of being turned to stone and coming back ("I could hear everything"). It counts as one of the Envoy's proofs in Ch20 and can be played for Juno in Ch10.

**Cliffhanger:** A Hollow broadcast hijacks every screen in Nadir. It isn't the Mourner. It's **Sable**, unmasked mouth, cheerful: *"The Mourner's gone soft. The Hammer hasn't. Cindervault's next. Light for everyone, right?"*

## CHAPTER 8 — "The Light Stone" (R13 → R14 → Cindervault, Gym 7; the Caldera Crisis)

**Location:** R14 Lake Sallow (Surf), **Lantern Isle** (optional, `:BEACH`), the road up into **Ashkaar**, **Cindervault** (`:VOLCANIC`), **Mount Cinder**, **Ashkaarfall** (668), R15 (`:ASHEN_WASTE` after the crisis).

**Borderland:** the land climbs out of Nadir's runoff into **Ashkaar, the Dragonfire Dominion**: black basalt, red lacquer banners and dragon-riders overhead. Oaths are sworn over braziers at every gate. Lying to a Dominion guard is a crime, and an early NPC teaches this ("Say true or say nothing, stranger").

**Arrival.** Cindervault is a forge-city inside Mount Cinder's caldera. Lava-channels for quenching, smithies stacked up the crater walls, the Hearthguild's bells.
- Warden **Brann Holloway** is a giant, booming and sentimental. He drops his hammer to bear-hug Larch, an old friend, and then the PC for good measure.
- **Two crises:**
  1. **Meridian's geothermal tap** at the caldera floor is overdrawing the Flame Plate's Imprint. The magma is rising, and the forges run too hot to work.
  2. The **Dominar's envoys** are in town demanding the **Light Stone**, the Hearthguild's ancestral relic (Reshiram), "for the throne of Ashkaar, as is right." Brann refuses on Concordance grounds.
- **First appearance of Sigrun Ashfall**, the Dragon Warden. She arrives on a Salamence to mediate between Brann and her father's envoys. She is fierce and curt, and she treats the PC with frank appraisal: "So you're the ferry child everyone's whispering about. Hm. Smaller than I expected." She calls the Dominar "my father" exactly once, in a tone that ends conversations.

**Gym 7 (`:VOLCANIC`).** Thematic note: *a forge you can stoke or quench, changing the field's heat level as you go.* Brann: "Some things you forge to last. Some things you forge to *break* right — that's the harder craft." His ace is **Coalossal**.

**The Caldera Crisis (set piece).** The tap's pressure spikes, and the caldera will erupt within the hour. Three groups converge on the caldera floor:
- **Wick's Hollow faithful**, with a plan to *vent* the pressure by quenching the Imprint with Lake Sallow water piped through the old lava-channels. It's safe but drastic: it turns the caldera to ash for a season.
- **Dr. Percival Quent's** Meridian team, who want to install "dampers" and keep the tap running. **Meridian admin battle: Quent** (`MERIDIAN_QUENT`), giggling: "Oh, it's *you*. The null reading! Hold still, I've only got one probe."
- **The Hammer:** a Bereaved squad planting a Splitter on the tap to cause an eruption "Meridian can't cover up".
- **CHOICE `:ch8_caldera`:**
  - **Help Wick vent.** The battle field transitions `:VOLCANIC` → `:ASHEN_WASTE`. The eruption is averted and the forges go cold for months, and Brann grumbles about it for a season. Hollow Standing goes up, Meridian Standing down. R15 becomes ash.
  - **Help Quent's dampers.** The eruption is averted, Meridian Standing goes up and the tap keeps running. The Bleed at Ashkaarfall grows (map state), and Brann's Bond goes up ("You kept my forges lit").
  - **Shut the tap down by force** (a harder fight vs Quent plus Directorate). The eruption is averted, Accord Standing goes up and the Dominar is impressed (`DOMINAR` respect +1). Meridian Standing drops hard.
- **Game over `GO_LET_THEM_LISTEN` "Let Them Listen".** Trigger: a Hammer bomber hands the PC the detonator and says "Do it. They'll finally listen." The PC accepts. Foreshadowing: the Field Notes entry on Shatters, and the Lowlight's glass. *Epitaph: "They listened. There was no one left to tell."*

**Aftermath: the Light Stone.**
- After the crisis, the Dominar's envoys try to seize the Light Stone in the confusion, and Sigrun stops them herself.
- **CHOICE `:ch8_lightstone`:**
  - Back Brann in keeping it under the Hearthguild. The Dominar's grudge grows.
  - Suggest it go to Sigrun at Wyrmrest, neutral ground under a Warden: Sigrun's Bond up, Accord Standing up. This seeds `DOMINAR_SWAYED`, because the Dominar respects his daughter's claim more than Brann's.
  - Say it should go to the Dominar ("it's his people's"): Ashkaar's favor goes up and Accord Standing drops. Brann is hurt, and in Act V he won't release the Flame Plate "for a man who gave the Light Stone away" (his Consent refusal). Late payoff: Reshiram's trial at Wyrmrest is harder.

**Revelation.** Brann, drunk and maudlin over chestnuts, lets slip one sentence: "I reset the Crown's stone myself, y'know. After the accident. Ten years back. It was smaller than it should've been." Then he catches himself: "Ach, I'm an old fool. Forget it." The PC's shard throbs once. (Larch notices the PC wince.)

**Cliffhanger:** The Dominar's herald reads a proclamation in the caldera square: Ashkaar holds Zenith responsible for the "assault on the Flame" and **closes the eastern passes to Meridian**. War-drums. Sigrun, watching her father's banner, says nothing at all.

## CHAPTER 9 — "The Iron Conclave" (R15 → R16 Ironway → Ironreach, Gym 8; K4; the Conclave; the Mourner unmasked)

**Location:** R15, **R16 Ironway** (`:MOUNTAIN`), **Ironreach** (Nadir, `:COLOSSEUM`), the **Iron Pit**, the Conclave galleries, the old Aurel mine shafts.

**Arrival.** Ironreach is Nadir's pit-fighting quarter, carved into Aurel-era mine shafts. The Fist Plate makes everyone strong, and arguments are settled in chalk circles. It's loud, sweaty and honest. Warden **Kaito Ren** (ex-Wardguard captain, laconic, wrapped hands) is the only former Accord soldier Nadir trusts.
- **The Conclave.** Sovereign Valen has called a rare **Accord Conclave** here, the first held in Nadir in a century, to vote on extending the Mandate realm-wide. Every available Warden is present.
  - **Introductions/reunions:** Tamsin, Dorian, Marisol, Rook, Sahar, Corvin, Brann, Sigrun, Rowan (who embraces Juno), and **Odessa Gale** herself, who delivers **Fly** in person from a skiff she lands on the Pit's rim ("Courier's privilege").
  - **First sightings** (Revision 5: these Wardens must be met before the Festival):
    - **Ysolde Ashgrave**, veiled, watches Kestrel across the Pit and leaves before he sees her.
    - **Nyx Calloway** is "slumming it" in the observers' gallery and buys the PC a drink they're too young for.
    - **Celestine Aubade**, barefoot and openly herself this time, smiles at the PC: "We've met. You didn't know it yet."
    - **Anja Sorrel** arrives with her brother's envoy and complains about the heat.
    - **Bastian Ore** pretends to be deaf to Hollis for the whole session.
  - Each Warden gets one line. Their full chapters come later.
  - Chancellor Hollis presides.
  - Kings' envoys sit in the "observers' gallery", and the Elowen and Ashkaar envoys refuse to sit next to each other.

**The Ironreach Open, K4.** The Iron Pit's open tournament runs during the Conclave as entertainment. Nadir loves it. The PC fights through brackets of pit-fighters (trainer gauntlet) to the **final vs Kestrel (K4)**.
- Kestrel is here because rumor says the Mourner will strike the Conclave. He wants to be closest when it happens.
- The crowd chants for "the null kid".

**Gym 8 (`:COLOSSEUM`).** Thematic note: *the crowd's roar is a stat; earn it.* Kaito: "Stand up. Again." His ace is **Lucario**. After the battle, quietly: "A fist is a promise. Don't make one you won't keep."

**The attack.**
1. During the Mandate vote, the galleries go dark. **The Hammer** has rigged the Fist Plate's reliquary, which was brought up for the Conclave's oath, with Splitters. Sable's voice on the pit speakers: *"Vote yes on the Mandate. Go on. Give them the leash. We'll give you a reason."*
2. **Partner battle: PC + Kestrel vs Sable + Hammer Captain Brisk.** Sable escapes into the shafts.
3. The PC pushes up to the Pit's upper ring and finds **the Mourner** standing over the reliquary. He's not planting the charge; he's trying to *disarm* it. But the player doesn't know that yet.
4. **The Mourner battle** (`HOLLOW_MOURNER`). **Boss: Trevenant** (multi-bar), with a Shiftry and Cacturne escort. The Mourner fights *defensively*. He keeps saying "Not now — you don't understand — get BACK."
5. **The partial Shatter.** The charge goes off anyway, and half the Fist Plate's Imprint bursts. The field in the reliquary chamber flips to `:BLEED`. Glass races across the stone toward the gallery, where **Juno** is shielding a child.
6. **The Mourner throws himself in the way.** His Trevenant's roots tear up through the floor to wall off the glass, and his mask takes the burst and **cracks in half**.
7. Juno, three meters away: "...Uncle Rowan?"
8. Rowan, bleeding and half-glassed at the shoulder: *"Juno. Don't look at me like that. Look at the flowers. Look at what they're standing on."*
9. The PC is the only one who can walk into the Bleed to pull Rowan out. Everyone, Valen included, watches them do it.

**CHOICE `:ch9_teo_sable` (the act's big choice).** Through the smoke the PC sees two things at once:
- **Teo Reyes** is pinned under fallen scaffolding at the Bleed's edge. He came with Rowan to stop the Hammer, and he is Fixing fast.
- **Sable** is running for a Meridian service lift with a comm-device in her hand.

The PC has time for one:
- **Save Teo** (`TEO_SAVED`). Teo lives, glassed fingers and all, and Marisol's Bond goes way up. Sable escapes clean, and her Meridian link has to be proven later and at higher cost (Ch13 via Ilsa/Nyx).
- **Chase Sable** (`IRONREACH_EVIDENCE`). A short chase battle through the shafts. The PC tears the comm-device from her belt: Meridian Special Accounts encryption, and a voice on it saying "Kroll, report." **Teo dies** in the Bleed, half-glass, reaching for his sister. Marisol's line in Ch13: *"He was nineteen. Nineteen. Tell me again how the Hollow are the monsters."*

**Aftermath.**
- **Meridian Directorate** officers take Rowan into **Meridian custody**, not the Accord's, under the Mandate, which just passed in the chaos. Marchetti: "Mandate jurisdiction, Sovereign." Valen allows it.
- Kestrel stares at Rowan's face for a long time. "...It's not her." He doesn't know whether he's relieved. He walks away into the shafts.
  - If the PC warned him in Ch6: "You said it might not be. I hate that you were right."
- Juno sits on the Pit's floor in the glass dust. She won't speak to anyone except, if `:ch3_juno_talk` was kind, the PC.
- **CHOICE `:ch9_rowan_words`:** as Rowan is led away, he looks to the PC. Answer:
  - "Why?": he says, "Ask the Drowned Crown."
  - "I'll look after her": he says, "I know. That's why I'm not afraid."
  - Silence: he says, "You saw what I saw. In the Lowlight. Didn't you?"

  Each sets a small Rowan Bond and colors his tribunal in Ch13.

**Optional thread: cameo sidequest "Two Lucarios" (Garret).** See `design/CAMEOS.md`. In the Ironreach Open's undercard, a gruff stranger with a Lucario asks every pit-fighter about a missing girl named Eden. It's available after the Conclave, in the shafts below the Pit.

**Revelation.** Valen crosses the ruined Pit to the PC, whose shoulder is dusted with glass that isn't hurting them. He looks at the glass, then at the PC.

> *"Come to Verdance. There's a seat to fill — and I think you should see what it costs."*

He leaves. Brann, at his elbow, looks back at the PC with an expression of pure grief.

**Game over `GO_PULL_THE_NAIL` "Pull the Nail".** Available only if Hollow Standing is high. Before the attack, Sable approaches the PC in the shafts: "You can walk in the Bleed. You could plant it where no one else can reach." Accepting leads to the Shatter in full, and Juno is Fixed. Foreshadowing: Wick's Ch7 line ("he wants to carry them home") and Sable's broadcast. *Epitaph: "You pulled the nail. The whole house came down."*

**INTERLUDE II — "The Bride Tree"** (playable as **Rowan**, as a flashback that plays *after* the unmasking).
- The dawn before the Conclave. Verdance's Heartgarden. The player controls Rowan as he waters the **Bride Tree**.
- Gentle walk-and-talk with the tree, which is Elin: "The roses are coming in again. They don't stop, love. They never stop."
- Mini-scene: he shows the healed Phantump from Ch1, now a Trevenant, the bark mask, and the lily he picks from her branches every time.
- It ends with him putting on the mask: "One more. Then I'll find the other way. I promise."

**END OF ACT II.** Title card: *III — Fault Lines.*
