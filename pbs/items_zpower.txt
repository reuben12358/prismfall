# Pokémon Prismfall - Z-Power items (Essentials v21.1 items format, merged as PBS/items_zpower.txt).
# Owner: Prismfall Z-Power (design/EEVEE_OCTAVE.md section 5). The crystals are slivers of the Crown's glass from the
# Glassfall, each tuned by the Prism Choir to one Imprint; held, they are never used up and can't be taken. SPINE Rev 23:
# the player's Warden's ring (the base MEGARING, from Bastian at 13 badges) wakes them as it wakes heartstones; the old
# Z-Power Ring stays defined only for saves that hold it (nobody hands it out). NPC trainers only need the crystal.
# Flag ZCrystal marks them unlosable in battle.
