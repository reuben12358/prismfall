### 1.2 Markdown Rendering Bugs in `dist_web/codex.html`
1. **179 bulleted/numbered lists rendered as flattened `<p>` walls!**
   - Python-Markdown (`markdown.Markdown`) strictly requires a blank line before `- ` / `1. ` lists (unlike GitHub-Flavored Markdown). Across `00_OVERVIEW.md`, `CHAPTER_BEATS.md`, `01_WORLD_LORE.md`, `WORLD_MAP.md`, `02_FACTIONS.md`, `03_CAST.md`, `04_ACT_I.md`, etc., **179 lists** follow a prose line with no blank line and get mashed into single `<p>` paragraphs (e.g., `01_WORLD_LORE.md`: `"Public articles (taught in every schoolroom): 1. Every Plate..."` all on one line).
   - **Fix in `tools/build_codex.py:render()`:** Run a simple pre-pass outside fenced code blocks (` ``` `) that inserts a blank line before any list start (`^[-*+]\s+` or `^\d+\.\s+`) or pipe-table header (`^\|.+\|\n\|[\s:-]+\|`) whose preceding line is non-blank prose (not already a list/table/blockquote/heading).
2. **Broken Markdown tables rendering as raw `|...|` pipes in `codex.html`:**
   - `story/24_SCRIPT_CH5.md:608-615` (Cutscene SH-1 storyboard table) — **currently broken in `dist_web/codex.html`!**
   - `story/13_CUTSCENES.md:129-130` (beam clash storyboard table) — **currently broken in `dist_web/codex.html`!**
   - `story/27_SCRIPT_CH9.md:511-518` (Cutscene IR-1 storyboard table) — **will break as soon as Ch9 is added!**
   - Adding blank lines in those three markdown files AND adding the table pre-pass in `build_codex.py` fixes all three permanently.

### 1.3 HTML / CSS / JS UX & Readability Improvements (`tools/build_codex.py:83-138`)
1. **Sticky `<nav>` overlaps every jumped-to heading (`scroll-margin-top` missing).**
   - `<nav>` is `position: sticky; top: 0` and wraps 27+ links into 2–3 rows (~110–150px tall on desktop). Neither `.chapter` nor `h1/h2/h3` has `scroll-margin-top`, so clicking any chapter link scrolls the heading right underneath the sticky nav bar.
   - **Fix:** Better yet, move `<nav>` on wide screens (`>= 1080px`) into a **240px left sticky sidebar** (grouped into *Story Bible*, *Playable Scripts*, *Walkthrough & Reference*, with active-chapter highlight via `IntersectionObserver`), and keep a compact collapsible `<details>` chapter drawer + `scroll-margin-top: 72px` on narrower screens.
2. **No search/filter across 213k+ words (235k+ with Ch9–10).**
   - A 1.52 MB single-page HTML document really needs a lightweight client-side filter/jump box (at minimum filtering the sidebar TOC + chapter `h2`/`h3` sub-headings and offering a jump-to-match search bar).
3. **Dead dark/light theme hook (`build_codex.py:95`).**
   - CSS defines `:root[data-theme=light]` and `:root[data-theme=dark]`, yet there is no theme toggle button or `<script>` in `codex.html`. Add a small top-right light/dark toggle button persisted in `localStorage`.
4. **Prism SVG mobile legibility (`build_codex.py:58-72`).**
   - At `viewBox="0 0 1200 600"` with `17px` font at `x="1010"`, on a 375px phone screen the 18 gym city labels shrink to `~5px` and become completely unreadable. Either increase font size / tighten the ray endpoints (`M240 300 L820 y`, text at `x="835"` at `21px` font) or provide a responsive compact layout under `max-width: 760px`.
5. **Script & cross-reference readability:**
   - The Codex currently prints 958 `[portrait: NAME, expr]` tags and 21 raw `\PN` tokens as plain text inside `<pre><code>` blocks. Rendering `\PN` as `<span class="pc">[Player]</span>` (or styled monospace) and lightly styling `[portrait: ...]` / `[bubble: ...]` lines in `build_codex.py` makes the 80,000+ words of scripts dramatically nicer to read without changing the source markdown.
   - Optionally rewrite inline file references (`story/09_MYSTERIES_FORESHADOWING.md`, `design/CHAPTER_BEATS.md`, `03_CAST`, etc.) into real internal `#anchor` links (`#mysteries`, `#chapter-beats`, `#cast`).
   - Add a **Spoiler toggle / banner** for the Walkthrough, Mysteries, Choices & Endings, and Ruin Route chapters (e.g. a subtle red/amber pill in the nav).

### 3.2 Honest Editorial & Game-Design Pushback (Where Gemini Disagrees / Recommends Changes)

#### A. Ch4–10 Story Scripts & Pacing Pushback
1. **Ch4 (`23_SCRIPT_CH4.md:303-322`) — "RPG Time-Freeze While a Child Drowns":**
   - Right now, Pella's punt is already hurtling straight toward the Guild Mill's lethal wheel (`L304`: `"She'll go under the wheel!"`), yet the player stops to fight a **double battle against two Bereaved** AND THEN a **full 3-Pokémon admin battle against Teo** (`L315`), while Teo is standing *right at the mill-race gate* somehow not seeing a screaming child in the water until after he loses.
   - **Pushback / Fix:** Keep the Bereaved skirmish as the race to the intake/towpath, then have **Teo fight you in the dark/mist thinking you're a Meridian runner trying to arrest him or re-jam the sluice — BEFORE Pella's punt rounds the bend into the lamplight**. Right as Teo loses, Marisol's shout and Pella's punt sweep into sight from upriver, and Teo freezes (`"There wasn't anyone on the water—"`) and grabs the chain beside you. Same beats, same two battles, zero broken urgency!
2. **Ch6 (`25_SCRIPT_CH6.md:207-238`) — `:ch6_drillsite`'s `:sanctuary` is a strictly dominant "Golden Third Option":**
   - Compare the three choices at the Deep-works:
     - `:hollow`: tough Directorate battle + Directorate ambushes in Ch7.
     - `:directorate`: battle + you help re-collar fleeing Pokémon + the caravan boy is arrested anyway + you lose Sahar's Act V Consent.
     - `:sanctuary`: **zero battle**, the child goes free, ALL the Drilbur and Ochre escape, Sahar thanks you, Accord Standing rises, and there are zero negative consequences!
   - **Pushback / Fix:** Give `:sanctuary` a real moral/political cost so the choice stays genuinely grey like Ch4. For example: Sahar's Sanctuary law protects *the child of the city*, **not** the "Meridian assets" — so the boy goes free under Sahar's cloak, *while Meridian handlers still drag half the collared Drilbur back down the shaft* (making the player choose between saving the Pokémon via `:hollow` or saving the child cleanly via `:sanctuary`), OR invoking Sanctuary forces Sahar to expose herself publicly so the Directorate seals the Mural Gallery/Dig Lab until after Ch13.
3. **Ch7 (`26_SCRIPT_CH7.md:158-163, 231-242, 312`) — Three small polish gaps:**
   - **`:ask_corvin` branch (`L158-163`) drops Juno's follow-up:** Juno asks you to ask Corvin for her, Corvin refuses (`"I treat hands. I don't ask whose."`), and we never see you return to Juno. Add 2 lines back in the dispensary (`"...He wouldn't say. Good. He's a good doctor."`) to close the scene.
   - **`GO_SWEET_TOOTH` missability (`L226-243`):** Make sure at least one Sweet Market vendor remains repeatable after Ch7 (until Pecharunt is defeated in Ch18) so players chasing all 23 Game Overs for the Ruin Route can't permanently miss `GO_SWEET_TOOTH` if they declined offers 1–2.
   - **Ama's weight (`L312`):** `"She's light as a lamp"` slightly misses the uncanny horror of a child turning to crystalline salt — `"Too light for nine, except where the salt's taken hold, heavy and cold as stone"` hits harder.
4. **Ch9 (`27_SCRIPT_CH9.md:424, 443-472`) — Boss Gauntlet Frustration + Brann's Vote:**
   - **No heal & brutal whiteout reset (`L443 → L468 → L471 → L545`):** Going straight from Sable+Brisk (tag) → multi-bar Mourner → Sable chase (`:chase_sable`) without a heal, AND restarting all the way back at `"The Vote"` if you lose to Mourner (`L471`), will make Hard-mode players tear their hair out. Add an automatic quick heal before climbing to `264` (e.g., Corvin tossing a spray from the rubble / Kestrel's spare potions) and set a checkpoint at `264`'s entrance so losing to Mourner restarts right at `264`.
   - **Brann raising his hand first for Meridian's Mandate (`L424`):** Right after Ch8 — where Ashkaar's Dominar closed the passes against Zenith and Brann may have just praised you for shutting Meridian's tap by force (`L134`) — seeing Brann raise his hand *first* to hand Meridian realm-wide Mandate powers feels off-character. Have **Magnus** raise first, then **Anja** slowly, then **Brann** only after glancing at Valen (or abstain if `:ch8_caldera == :shutdown`!).
5. **Ch10 (`28_SCRIPT_CH10.md:380-381`) — Loyal Three `:hesitate` Gauntlet Tuning:**
   - On `:hesitate`, fighting Okidogi → Munkidori → Fezandipiti back-to-back solo **with +1 bar added to each** means **9 boss bars on Standard** and **12 boss bars + 2v1 doubles on Hard**! Losing Ogerpon as your partner is *already* a steep, fair penalty — either heal the party between the three bosses or drop the extra `+1 bar` stack on `:hesitate` (keeping 2 bars Standard / 3 bars Hard, solo vs with Ogerpon) so `:hesitate` feels hard rather than punishingly tedious.
6. **Ch8 (`design/CHAPTER_BEATS.md:29` & `story/22_SCRIPT_CH8.md`) — The Missing Bridge:**
   - Ch8 is now the **only unscripted chapter in Acts I–II** (Ch0–7 and Ch9–10 are all done!), leaving a hole between Ch7's `"Cindervault's next"` cliffhanger and Ch9's `"The passes are shut to Zenith"` opening. Prioritizing the real `22_SCRIPT_CH8.md` (and retiring the old Ring-Day stub) before drafting Ch11 will make Acts I–II completely continuous!

#### B. `design/FIELD_LOGIC_AUDIT.md` Pushback
1. **Stormfront's `"No sun"` + `"2 Sunny Days -> Rainbow"` contradiction (`FIELD_LOGIC_AUDIT.md:274, 276` & `115_Field_Stormfront.rb:78-84`):**
   - The audit cut `Sky Drop` surfacing `Underwater` for the exact reason that a move shouldn't both *fail* and trigger a transition — yet **Stormfront still has that exact contradiction**, and Trainer Round 4 (`f2a8e05`) proved **Odessa's own Noivern NEVER casts Sunny Day** because the AI avoids blocked weather moves!
   - **Recommendation:** Either (a) keep `2 Sunny Days → Rainbow` and explicitly override AI scoring for `:even_if_failed` field transitions (and change the 1st-cast message so it feels like progress, not a failure: *"A shaft of sun pries at the storm clouds!"* rather than *"swallowed the sunlight!"*), OR (b) trigger Stormfront → Rainbow via **2 prismatic/light moves** (e.g. `Dazzling Gleam`, `Flash`, `Solar Beam`, `Aurora Beam`, `Morning Sun`) or **Clear Skies (`Defog` + `Sunny Day`)** so players aren't asked to click a "failed" move twice.
2. **Mirror Glass evasion/accuracy-drop amplification (`FIELD_LOGIC_AUDIT.md:67-68`):**
   - Giving `Double Team` / `Minimize` **+2 evasion** per click and `Flash` / `Sand Attack` / `Smokescreen` **-2 accuracy** per click invites classic fangame RNG-cheese frustration (both for players cheesing Reshiram/Kyogre and if any AI trainer uses it). Mirror Glass is *already* super cool with beam ×1.5 sure-hit, reflection counters ×1.5, extended screens/Aurora Veil, Electric insulation, and sound-shattering. Consider **dropping or softening** the evasion/accuracy bonus (e.g. keeping evasion at normal +1, or letting Mirror Glass reflect stat-drop glare via Mirror Coat/screens instead).
3. **Add `Brick Break` & `Psychic Fangs` to Glass Dunes' crack list (`FIELD_LOGIC_AUDIT.md:74`):**
   - Moves whose entire identity is shattering barriers (`Brick Break`, `Psychic Fangs`, `Raging Bull`) are the #1 move players will instinctively click on **Mirror Glass** — having them deal 1 crack (or 2 on Mirror Glass, like Rock Smash) rewards player intuition!
4. **`Dragon's Den`'s `"Amulet Coin holders can't be statused"` (`FIELD_LOGIC_AUDIT.md:377`):**
   - Clever flavour ("the hoard protects its own"), but fails the creator's *"could a player guess why this happens here?"* test unless called out loudly in Field Notes and in Wyrmrest NPC dialogue (e.g. a dragon-tamer hinting *"Bring gold into a dragon's den, and the hoard treats you as its own."*).
5. **Minor `Z_MOVES.md` vs `SPINE.md` Rev 23 check:**
   - `SPINE.md` Rev 23 says *"All 18 type crystals are spread from 13 badges on"*, while `design/Z_MOVES.md` places 5 early teaser crystals (`Icium Z` at Badge 2, `Steelium Z` at 4, `Rockium Z` & `Grassium Z` at 8, `Flyinium Z` at 10) that *"ring and wait"* until Bastian's Ring at Badge 13. **Gemini actually AGREES with `Z_MOVES.md` here** — finding silent, mysterious Choir-glass slivers early mirrors Reborn's early Mega Stones and makes Bastian's Ring-Day reveal feel awesome! Just add a 1-line confirmation note in `SPINE.md` Rev 23 (or ask the creator to rubber-stamp the 5 early teaser crystals) so `SPINE.md` and `Z_MOVES.md` match verbatim.
