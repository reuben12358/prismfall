## 1. Forensic Diagnosis: Why Ch9 Gave Literal Goosebumps vs. Why Waves 2 & 3 Failed

Creator feedback drew an unmistakable line between what works and what fails in RPG Maker XP / Pokémon Essentials story cinema:

- **Scene 2 (Ch9 — Trevenant Ingrain & Mourner Unmasking, proof `96`):** Delivered genuine chills because **every second was grounded, physical, character-first pixel-art acting**: the Mourner sprints down a crimson-lit hall, pitches a ballistic Dusk Ball, Trevenant physically materializes in rich dark-bark pixel art (`act_ch9_trevenant_ingrain.png`) and slams gnarled living roots (`pf_vfx_ingrain_roots.png`) across the hallway to shield a frozen Juno from razor crystal shards, and Rowan takes a stray shard directly on his ash-bark mask until it splits to reveal his face.
- **Waves 2 & 3 (Ch11 Kyogre, Ch19 WAR-1, Ch15 Glassfall, Ch21 Arceus, proofs `97` & `98`):** Failed catastrophically for **four structural reasons**:
  1. **Procedural Python MS-Paint Geometry:** Instead of real pixel art, Wave 2/3 VFX sheets were drawn with Python geometric primitives — a cartoonish neon red-and-yellow Sauron oval eye pasted over a small pond (`pf_vfx_ice_fissure`), blue trapezoid rectangles (`pf_vfx_origin_pulse`), scanline sine zigzags (`pf_vfx_deluge_rain`), flat straight cyan horizontal bars + solid white/mint circles (`pf_vfx_origin_beam`, `pf_vfx_blue_flare_beam`, `pf_vfx_beam_clash_core`), squiggle lines + flat pastel-green polygons (`pf_vfx_glassfall_curtain`), and giant neon purple wheels + a vertical pink rectangle (`pf_vfx_arceus_18locks`, `pf_vfx_prism_helix_18`).
  2. **Frozen Overworld Dolls:** Human cast members (Anja, Juno, Eirik, Hrothgar, Sigrun, Morwen, Young Voss, Valen, Ilsa) stood motionless in straight horizontal rows at the bottom edge of the screen like bowling pins while MS-Paint shapes flashed above them.
  3. **Zero Dramatic Camera Grammar:** Every beat stayed trapped in the flat distant top-down grid camera — never once cutting into Rejuvenation V14's signature **90° Banquet-style side-view / dramatic close-up pixel-art plates** at the emotional climax.
  4. **Speedrun Pacing (~19s per catastrophe):** Major emotional turning points (Kyogre breathing beneath Rimeholt, two kingdoms vitrifying the Glass Dunes, Morwen sacrificing her name to shield a five-year-old child, Valen turning to light in front of his estranged daughter) raced past in 18–21 seconds with no room for quiet dread, human vulnerability, or aftermath.

### The Six Permanent Laws of Prismfall Rejuvenation V14 Cinema

1. **ZERO Procedural MS-Paint Geometry — Ever Again:** Every VFX sprite, acting sheet, and cutscene plate is **100% Pure `generate_image` crisp 2× DS pixel art**, downsampled cleanly onto a native 1× pixel grid with zero dithering, upscaled strictly `2×` `NEAREST`, and mathematically verified: **`exact2x=True, semi=0, orphans=0`**.
2. **Character-First Physical Acting:** People move with weight and emotion — running against crowds, hauling refugees up ladders, shielding faces from heat-blasts, gripping rain-slick rails, tearing off earmuffs, clutching shattered masks, collapsing on marble flagstones. Static standing lines are banned.
3. **Authentic B2W2 / Rejuv Pokémon Acting Sheets:** Legendaries and partner Pokémon act with physical weight and silhouette (`act_ch9_trevenant_ingrain`, `act_war1_primal_kyogre`, `act_war1_reshiram`, plus rebuilt `Arceus`, `Mismagius`, `Meloetta`, `Ogerpon`, `Loyal Three`, `Mega Sharpedo`, `Salamence`, `Giratina`). Never paste Python X-bars or geometric rings over Pokémon sprites.
4. **90° Banquet-Style Apex Cuts (`512×384` Native `exact2x` Plates):** At each cutscene's emotional peak, smoothly cut (via `flash_ramp` or hard `bgm_cut` beat) from the live top-down RMXP tilemap into a bespoke **`512×384` (`256×192` native × 2) crisp 2× DS pixel-art 90° side-view illustration**, anchored directly to Prismfall's canonical `graphics/Trainers/` & `graphics/Portraits/` cast sheets. Animate the plate subtly in-engine (slow camera pan/zoom, live pixel rain/ash/mote particles on `@vfx_vp`, subtle camera shake, and `PortraitBox` dialogue) before cutting cleanly back to the visibly scarred live tilemap!
5. **Dragged-Out Breathing Pacing (`30–40s` per scene):** Give every major scene a proper four-act rhythm:
   - *Act I (0–9s):* Grounded human stakes & escalating unease on the live map (dialogue, small physical tells, environmental cues).
   - *Act II (9–19s):* Physical crisis & character action (running, rescuing, throwing Poké Balls, Pokémon entering/bracing).
   - *Act III (19–30s):* `0.30–0.45s` dead-silence `bgm_cut` audio vacuum → **90° Banquet-style apex pixel plate** with live overlays & gut-punch emotional line.
   - *Act IV (30–40s):* Return to the **visibly transformed / scarred live tilemap** with lingering silence, weather shift, and human aftermath.
6. **Clean Engine Primitive (`017_AdvancedAnimations.rb` Extension — Spec Only, Ready for Post-Approval Merge):**
   Add one lightweight, leak-free helper to `Prismfall::AdvancedAnimations::Engine`:
   ```ruby
   # Renders a crisp 512x384 exact2x=True 90° pixel-art apex plate inside @ui_vp (below PortraitBox & letterbox)
   # while keeping live @vfx_vp pixel overlays (rain, snow, ash, light motes) and camera pan/shake active!
   eng.show_cut_plate(
     "Graphics/Pictures/cut_ch11_kyogre_90deg",
     fade_in: 0.20, hold: 5.5, fade_out: 0.35,
     pan: [0, 0] => [-10, -4], zoom: [1.0, 1.06], shake: 2
   ```
