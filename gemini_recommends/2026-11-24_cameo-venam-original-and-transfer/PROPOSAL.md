## 2. Restored Original Pokémon Rejuvenation Venam (`VENAM` / `FESTIVAL_VENAM`)

### Canonical Design Verification
Researched directly against official *Pokémon Rejuvenation* in-game V13/V13.5 sprites (VS cut-in & battle front), official Zumi (`@honnojis.art`) Chapter 1 promo artwork, and the official `pokemonrejuvenation.com` character avatar:
- **Anchored Version:** **Classic / V13.5 Original Venam** — the iconic purple-haired punk design recognized across 10+ years of *Pokémon Rejuvenation*:
  - **Hair:** Wild, messy, shoulder-to-mid-back flared **royal violet-purple** hair (`#8C52E0` base, `#B884FF` highlight, `#562898` shadow) pinned with **two bright hot-pink X-bow hairclips** (`#E638B2` / `#FF9AE2`) on the left and right sides of her head.
  - **Face:** Warm **chestnut-brown eyes** (`#542814` / `#964E26`), feisty angled brows, and her signature **cheeky tongue-out punk smirk (`:P`)**.
  - **Outfit:** Sleeveless **charcoal-black fitted tank top** (`#2E2C3E`) with a bold **crimson-red V-chevron** (`#CE2238`) across the chest; **crisp white pleated mini-skirt** (`#FAF9FF`) featuring a bold **crimson-red zig-zag pattern**; **knee-high black socks with horizontal crimson stripes**; **black punk strap shoes**.
- *Historical Version Note:* In March 2026 (`V14`), Zumi previewed a refreshed Ch1 redesign keeping the exact same purple flared hair, twin hot-pink bow clips, brown eyes, and white zig-zag mini-skirt while adding an off-shoulder charcoal top, spiked choker, fingerless gloves, studded pink belt, and chunky boots. We deliberately anchored Prismfall's cameo on **Classic / V13.5 Original Venam** per Reuben's directive to use her iconic original look (and to maximize visual separation from `PUNK_ZARA`'s leather-jacket/choker/studded-belt aesthetic).

### Custom B2W2 Restrained 16-Frame Battle Animation (`Trainers/FESTIVAL_VENAM.png`)
Engineered to match Prismfall's Game Freak B2W2 PWT movement economy (`CHAMPION_VALEN` gold standard — firmly anchored legs/boots at `bottom_y = 156` across all 16 frames, zero choppy redraws, ONE unmistakable character-defining signature beat):
- **Frames 0–2 (`Ready Stance`):** Anchored wide punk stance facing 3/4 left, right hand propped on hip, left hand lowered at waist gripping her Poké Ball casually with a confident closed-lip smirk.
- **Frames 3–4 (`Anticipation Toss`):** Left arm bends smoothly up to chest height, tossing the Poké Ball lightly upward off her open palm with an eager playful grin.
- **Frames 5–9 (`Signature Fingertip Spin Peak`):** Direct animated homage to her iconic Rejuvenation VS cut-in & Ch1 artwork — left index finger raised high balancing & spinning the Poké Ball (`f6`/`f8` 1px buoyant lift + bright specular glint, `f7` spin highlight shift) while flashing her signature cheeky `:P` tongue-out punk smirk!
- **Frames 10–11 (`Crisp Catch`):** Smooth return catch mid-frame.
- **Frames 12–15 (`Confident Hero Hold`):** Anchored ready pose with a natural 1-frame blink on `f14` and crisp static hold on `f15`.
