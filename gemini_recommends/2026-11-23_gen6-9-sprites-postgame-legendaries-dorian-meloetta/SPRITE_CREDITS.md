# Pokémon Prismfall — Full National Dex (`#001–#1025` + 395 Forms) Animated B2W2 Battle Sprite Credits

All `2,663` newly installed / upgraded Gen 6–9, Regional, Primal, Mega, and Alternate Form animated battle strips in Pokémon Prismfall's Full National Dex pack (`/home/antigravity/pf_engine_run/ebdx_gen6_9_animated_pack_full/`) are **100% 2D Gen 5 B2W2-style pixel art** sourced exclusively from open-source non-commercial Pokémon Essentials & Smogon community sprite projects. **Zero 3D model renders (`ani/`) were imported.**

Copy this attribution block into Prismfall's root `CREDITS.md` / in-game Credits screen when shipping the animated sprite pack.

---

## 1. Primary Open-Source Sprite Packs Used

### Open-Source EBDX Renovation Project 4.0 (ChromeValiant)
- **Repository:** `https://github.com/ChromeValiant/Pokemon-Essentials-21-With-Unofficial-EBDX`
- **Resource Page:** `https://eeveeexpo.com/resources/1831/`
- **Role in Prismfall:** Primary unified 2D Gen 5 B2W2-style horizontal strip compilation (`2,582 / 2,663` strips in the staged `--scope all` Full National Dex pack), integrating Lucidious89's Animated Pokémon System `[SPRITES]`, Smogon 2D Sprite Projects, Aronousqui's Mega Evolutions, mangalos810's animations, the Spanish *Sprites Animados* / *La Base de Sky* community project, and Ghasty_001's Gen 6–9 animations.
- **License / Terms:** Free for non-commercial Pokémon fan games with full artist credits preserved below.

### Pokémon Showdown `gen5ani` Buckets (Smogon BW Sprite Project)
- **CDN Buckets (strictly 2D `gen5ani*` only):** `https://play.pokemonshowdown.com/sprites/gen5ani/`, `gen5ani-back/`, `gen5ani-shiny/`, `gen5ani-back-shiny/`
- **Community Thread:** `https://www.smogon.com/forums/threads/smogon-sprite-project.3647722/`
- **Role in Prismfall:** Direct strictly-2D GIF → EBDX strip unique-frame upgrader (`81 / 2,663` strips in the staged `--scope all` pack), upgrading **Slurpuff** (`173f`), **Toxapex Back** (`159f`), **Tapu Lele** (`96f/120f`), **Origin Dialga Back** (`108f`), **Drakloak** (`105f`), **Clawitzer Back** (`99f`), **Dreepy Back** (`88f`), **Flabébé** (all 5 colors, `80f`), **Dragapult Back** (`76f`), **Floette** (`73f`), **Litleo** (`72f`), **Wishiwashi** (`70f`), **Pangoro** (`63f/38f`), **Carbink** (`62f`), **Alcremie** (9 primary cream Backs, `62f`), **Hoopa** (`61f`), **Toxtricity Back** (`60f`), **Galarian Articuno** (`27f`), **Honedge**, and **Malamar Back**.

### Animated Sprites by Ghasty001 (Ghasty_001)
- **Repository:** `https://github.com/Ghasty001/Animated_sprites_by_Ghasty001`
- **Role in Prismfall:** Hand-animated 2D Gen 6–9 / Hisuian / Mega B2W2 GIFs (integrated directly and via Renovation 4.0, including **Annihilape**, **Gholdengo** Back, **Dragapult** line, **Clawitzer**, **Dragalge**, **Heliolisk**, **Garganacl** line, **Arboliva** line, **Skeledirge** line, **Dachsbun** line, **Armarouge/Ceruledge**, **Kingambit**, **Clodsire**, **Sneasler**, **Wyrdeer**, **Hisuian Braviary/Qwilfish/Voltorb/Electrode/Sliggoo/Avalugg**, etc.).
- **License / Terms:** Explicitly *"free to use for other projects with credit"* (including base spriters credited below).

### Animated Pokémon System `[SPRITES]` (Lucidious89)
- **Resource Page:** `https://eeveeexpo.com/resources/1544/`
- **Role in Prismfall:** Core Gen 6–9 DLC + Regional + Mega + Alternate Form animated sprite compilation incorporated into Renovation 4.0 (also mirrored by `La-Base-De-Sky-Plugins/Sprites-Animados-DBK`).
- **Base Architecture Credits:** Lucidious89, based on the Generation 8 Pack by **Golisopod User** and Elite Battle: DX by **Luka S.J.** (ported to Essentials v21.1 by **Manurocker95**).
