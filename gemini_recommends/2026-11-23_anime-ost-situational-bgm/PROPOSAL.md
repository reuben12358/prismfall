## 1. The Golden Rule of Prismfall's Soundtrack Architecture

Pokémon Prismfall shines brightest when **each musical idiom plays to its absolute strength**:

1. **KEEP POKÉMON / RPG BATTLE MUSIC FOR POKÉMON BATTLES (ZERO Mismatched Anime Battle Swaps!):**
   - v1's entire 10-track `Boss & Rival Themes` battle-swap tier has been **100% removed**.
   - Wild encounters, Route Trainers, Gym Wardens, Rivals, Hollow/Meridian Grunts & Admins, Multi-Bar Legends, and Crownhaven Festival Brackets belong to punchy, loopable Pokémon/RPG battle music (GlitchxCity, Zumi, CatchDalgo, Octopath, Hollow Knight, Trails, PGR, Blue Archive).
   - All **4 Creator-Locked Slots** remain **100% untouched**:
     - 🔒 **`#2 Battle - Warden Nyx` (`LEADER_NYX`)** → GlitchxCity — *Corbeau Battle Theme (Remix)* *(Nyx gets Cowboy Bebop's **Goodnight Julia** strictly as her smoky non-battle Underveil walk-in!)*
     - 🔒 **`#4 Battle - Festival Final`** → GlitchxCity — *Champion Iris Battle Theme (Remix)*
     - 🔒 **`#1 Mood - Comedy / Team Compost Chaos`** → Blue Archive — *Unwelcome School* *(KonoSuba & Nichijou serve purely as complementary walk-in / everyday banter cues so Unwelcome School keeps its explosive punch!)*
     - 🔒 **`#3 Battle - Aurelian Voss` (`MERIDIAN_VOSS`)** → Punishing: Gray Raven — *Out of My Hands* *(Voss gets Bleach's **Treachery** strictly as his cold boardroom/elevator walk-in!)*
2. **DEPLOY ANIME OSTs WHERE THEY ELEVATE PRISMFALL TO GOD-TIER STORYTELLING:**
   - **Goofy, Quirky & Warm Character Walk-Ins (10 tracks):** Classic Pokémon `Encounter!` / `Looker` energy upgraded with anime precision for **Dorian, Team Compost, Wenlow, Martin ('I LEFT THE OVEN ON!'), Dex, Professor Larch, Juno, Captain Edda, Venam, and Odessa/Brann**.
   - **Serious, Intimidating & Haunting Character Walk-Ins (10 tracks):** Unforgettable pre-battle tension (`Theme of N` / `Cynthia's Piano` energy) when **Voss, The Mourner, Kestrel, Sable/Kroll, Champion Valen, Marshal Marchetti, Ysolde/Morwen, Archseer Dask, Cantor Vesper, or Warden Nyx** steps into the room — BEFORE snapping cleanly into authentic Pokémon battle music!
   - **Towns, Cities & Safe Havens (10 tracks):** Unforgettable sense of place across Elowen, Zenith, Ossaria, Nadir, Isenmar, Antumbra, and Crownhaven via Joe Hisaishi, Evan Call, Yoko Kanno, Toshio Masuda, Shiro Sagisu, Kow Otani, and [K]NoW_NAME.
   - **Routes, Wilderness & Dungeons (9 tracks):** Sunlit trails, enchanted forests, crystal caverns, desert mural vaults, volcanic seams, cold Meridian stealth, Glassfall Bleed anomalies, cloud crossings, and late-game war marches.
   - **Story Moods, `pf_hangout` Campfires, `15_COMEDY.md` Banter & Cutscenes (10 tracks):** Dedicated non-battle emotional peaks, cozy campfire bonding, dry town comedy, conspiracies, and epilogue credits.
   - **Pure Instrumental Dominance:** **42 / 49 (85%)** are **100% Pure Instrumental** (zero singing/lyrics), sitting cleanly behind RPG dialogue blips; only **7** atmospheric tracks use wordless choir/vocalise and are explicitly badged.

## 5. Implementation & Mastering Workflow (Zero Repo Bloat)

1. **Audition & Lock In:** Open `prismfall_anime_ost_audition.html`, filter across all 5 tabs (`Goofy & Warm Walk-Ins`, `Serious & Haunting Walk-Ins`, `Towns & Safe Havens`, `Routes & Dungeons`, `Moods, Comedy & Cutscenes`), mark `🔒 Lock In` / `✅ Approve` / `❌ Reject` (auto-saved under `localStorage` key `prismfall_anime_ost_reviews_v1`), and click **Export Lock-In Report**.
2. **Zero Battle Regression:** Keep every Pokémon battle slot in `design/AUDIO_SOUNDTRACK_PLAN.md` (`#88–#145`, plus Creator Locks `#2`, `#3`, `#4`) intact; wire locked anime walk-ins as `Theme - <Character>.ogg` via `pbBGMPlay` on character entrance events, restoring map BGM or snapping into Pokémon battle music cleanly at encounter start.
3. **Master via `master_track.py`:** Source personal copies locally, normalize to `-14.0 LUFS` (`-1.5 dBTP`, `44.1 kHz` stereo Ogg Vorbis `-q:a 6`) with sample-accurate `LOOPSTART` / `LOOPLENGTH` tags, and pair with `audio/gemini_cc0/BGS/*.ogg` using the RMXP volume ratios in Section 2.2. Commercial anime OST binaries are never committed to the public git repo.
