## 1. Executive Summary & High-Impact Situational Gaps Solved

Prismfall's v4.4 145-track blueprint (`design/AUDIO_SOUNDTRACK_PLAN.md`) has phenomenal town, route, and battle coverage (Pokémon Remixes, Rejuvenation, Octopath, Hollow Knight, Xenoblade, Trails), **but relies on just 6 reusable `Mood - *` slots (`#77–#82`) to carry 60 hours of peak cinema, heartbreak, conspiracies, campfire bonds, and Ruin Route psychological horror.**

This proposal curates **48 iconic, non-cliché anime OSTs** across **17 master composers** (**27 Pure Instrumental** + **21 Choir/Vocalise/Vocals**, transparently tagged) organized into **6 situational tiers** so every major beat in Velmora hits like theatrical anime:

1. **Dedicated Peak Cutscene Scoring (8 tracks):** Bespoke cinema pairings for all 5 Rejuvenation V14-grade Peak Setpieces (Ch5 Spire Shatter, Ch9 Uncle Rowan Unmasking, Ch11 Kyogre Awakening, Ch19 Glass Dunes Beam Clash, Ch21 Valen & Arceus Convergence, Ruin R7 Mirror-Pierce) plus Ch14 *The Ring Sings* (`:RD_1`) and Ch15 *Hollow Cathedral Echo*.
2. **Zero-Fatigue Emotional Range (8 tracks):** Splits `#79 Resting Grounds` into distinct emotional colors — stunned Lowlight devastation (*Across the Violet Sky*), unpreventable Sump Purge death (*Never Coming Back*), rainy Morwen guilt (*Samidare*), tender Bride Tree lament (*Sadness and Sorrow*), warm campfire bonds (*Time Flows Ever Onward* — **fills an outright soundtrack gap!**), solitary Unimprinted isolation (*Gatsu*), tragic farewell (*Call of Silence*), and healing Homecoming credits (*Here to Stay*).
3. **Surgical Conspiracy & Ruin Route Dread (8 tracks):** Upgrades Meridian scheming (*Treachery*, *Light's Theme*), Larch/Corvin clue deductions (*L's Theme A*), Arbiter Blueprint horror (*Kingdom of Predators*), urban false-flag panic (*Riot*), Glassfall Bleed uncanniness (*Sis Puella Magica!*), Mandate oppression (*The Power of All For One*), and cold lucid Ruin Route horror (*Restriction and Pledge*).
4. **Crystalline Wonder, Warm Hearths & Grand Festival Hype (7 tracks):** Adds Kevin Penkin's *Made in Abyss* wonder (*Hanezeve Caradhina*, *To the Abyss!*, *Old Stories*, *Tomorrow*), Evan Call's storybook hearth (*A Well-Earned Celebration*), Kow Otani's twilight serenity (*Free Bird*), and Yuki Hayashi's electrifying stadium entrance (*Above*) for Ch20's 40,000-spectator Opening Ceremony.
5. **Heroic Comebacks & War Gravitas (7 tracks):** Adds an explicit **Turning-the-Tide** slot (*You Say Run* for Ch10 Ogerpon's canopy drop and Ch21's 11-Lamp Ally Montage!), strategic war-room urgency (*Decisive Battle*), tragic marching grandeur (*Elegy of the Dynast*), Ch21 hijack intensity (*THE ANSWER*, *Voices of the Chord*), defiant wildcard (*Libera Me from Hell*), and Arceus's hushed blessing (*Soundscape to Ardor*).
6. **Optional Signature Boss & Rival Alternates (10 tracks):** High-fit optional alternates for Kestrel (*Knife to the Throat*, *EMIYA*), Valen (*Frieren the Slayer*), Theodric (*Zoltraak*), Echo of Morwen (*Symposium Magarum*), Mourner (*Credens Justitiam*), Archseer Dask (*The Battle is to the Strong*), Bound Arceus (*DARK ARIA <LV2>*), Dorian/Festival duels (*Virtuous Pope*), and Voss (*Stand Up Be Strong* / *Invasion* — strictly optional).

### 2.2 Pure Instrumental vs. Choir / Vocalise Discipline
- **27 / 48 tracks are 100% Pure Instrumental (zero singing, zero lyrics)** — safe for seamless looping under RPG dialogue and battle SFX.
- **21 / 48 tracks include iconic anime orchestral choir, conlang vocalise (*Kajiurago*, *Made in Abyss*), or theatrical cutscene vocals** (*Vogel im Käfig*, *DARK ARIA <LV2>*, *Call of Silence*, *Voices of the Chord*, *THE ANSWER*, *Libera Me from Hell*) — every single one is **badged explicitly** in both this document and the interactive studio, and is recommended primarily for **non-looping peak cutscenes, ending credits, or opt-in climax phases** rather than dialogue-heavy loops.

### 2.3 Fangame Asset & Licensing Transparency
Just like official Pokémon OST rips, Octopath Traveler, Hollow Knight, Xenoblade, and Punishing: Gray Raven tracks in `AUDIO_SOUNDTRACK_PLAN.md`, commercial anime OSTs remain copyright of their respective studios/labels (Aniplex, Kadokawa, Pony Canyon, VAP, Toho, Milan, Lantis, etc.). In non-commercial fangame development, **nothing is bundled into the public git repo** — creators source personal copies locally via the queries below and master them through `master_track.py` (`44.1 kHz` stereo Ogg Vorbis `-q:a 6`, `-14.0 LUFS`, `LOOPSTART`/`LOOPLENGTH` Vorbis tags). All 50 original CC0 ME/BGS/SE files in Prismfall remain 100% clean.

1. **Audition & Lock-In:** Open `prismfall_anime_ost_audition.html`, audition the `0:00 → 0:32` intros by situation tab, click **🔒 Lock In** / **✅ Approve** / **❌ Reject**, jot any beat-swap notes, and click **Export Lock-In Report** (`prismfall_anime_ost_lockin_report.md`).
2. **Additive Slot Expansion (Preferred for Gaps & Peak Cutscenes):** For brand-new situational slots that don't exist in v4.4 yet — such as `Mood - Campfire Bonds.ogg`, `Mood - Heroic Comeback.ogg`, `Mood - Solitary Burden.ogg`, `Mood - Meridian Boardroom.ogg`, `Festival - Opening Entrance.ogg`, and per-Peak-Cutscene `.ogv` audio beds (`pf_ch5_shatter`, `pf_ch9_unmasking`, `pf_ch11_kyogre_eye`, `pf_ch19_war_clash`, `pf_ch21_arceus_convergence`, `pf_ruin_mirror_pierce`) — add them as **new additive slots (`#152+`)** without touching a single existing v4.4 track.
