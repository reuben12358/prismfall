# Proposal: EBDX Bridge Particle Bleach Fix, Flooded Dunes Double-Flash, Portrait Emote Outlines & Tidelock SnapEdges

- **What:** Fix remaining live visual/engine bugs in EBDX Bridge, Flooded Dunes/Desert transitions, Portrait/Bubble rendering, and Tidelock Gym `SnapEdges` (verified still present after commits `e635be7`, `44f5207`, and `3a520c6`).
- **Why:**
  1. **EBDX `reconfigure` bleaches ambient `"pfx*"` particles to pure white (`plugins/Prismfall EBDX Bridge/003_Bridge_EBDX.rb:52-60, 234-240, 250-301`):** `refresh` tints particles via `spr.color = PrismfallEBDX.to_color(..., 255)` (L239). On any colour-fade `reconfigure(data, Color)` (L57), EBDX tweens `spr.color` on every `@sprites` entry down to `alpha = 0`, permanently wiping particle RGB tints to white for the rest of the battle (`prismfall_respawn` never restores `spr.color`). Also, `prismfall_spawn_art_layers` (L333) does not check `@focused` (unlike L259), and rescuing an error in `update` (L117-124) sets `@pf_particles = nil` which also halts `prismfall_update_art_layers` and `prismfall_update_flash`.
  2. **Double room flash on `FLOODED_DUNES -> DESERT (Stage 2)` (`plugins/Prismfall Field Effects/139_Field_FloodedDunes.rb:61-62`):** Calls `battle.change_field(:DESERT, "...superbloom!")` followed immediately by `battle.set_field_stage(2, "")`, firing **two consecutive transitions** (flashing dry Desert while printing Superbloom text, then flashing into Superbloom). Fix by adding an optional `stage: 1` arg to `Battle#change_field`.
  3. **Field Engine Minor Bugs:**
     - `003_FieldEffects_BattleState.rb:249-262`: `set_field_stage` skips `on_field_start`, leaving active Grassy Terrain alive when Desert drops from Stage 2 -> Stage 1.
     - `003_FieldEffects_BattleState.rb:295-296`: `BATTLE_RULES_WITH_VALUE` is missing v21.1 value rules (`"size"`, `"bgm"`, `"battlebgm"`, `"victorybgm"`, `"introtext"`, `"losetext"`, `"wintext"`, `"expall"`, `"switchstyle"`).
     - `004_FieldEffects_Engine.rb:413`: indirect `:damage_type` calls raw `Effectiveness.calculate` instead of `modify_effectiveness` (ignoring `:INVERSE`).
     - `112_Field_Colosseum.rb:84-90` + `003_Mega_Fields.rb:70-76`: Mega Evolution increments `st[:kos]`, causing Colosseum to announce *"Two down! The crowd is in a frenzy!"* with 0 KOs.
     - `007_FieldEffects_AI.rb`: AI doesn't score `:added_effects`, `:move_stat_boosts`, `:fixed_damage_mods`, or `:effect_chance_mods`.
  4. **Speech-bubble emote solid black boxes & pure-white portrait well (`plugins/Prismfall Cutscenes/014_Portraits.rb:380, 406, 435, 504`):** In `docs/playtest/portraits_bubble.png`, `:anger`, `:question`, `:exclaim`, and `:dots` draw opaque solid black rectangles (`C::Pix.rect(..., col(DARK))`) behind the emote instead of 1-art-px shaped outlines. Meanwhile, `Frame.cut` (L504) fills the 96x96 `FaceBox` center with pure `#F8F8F8` white, washing out pale hair/veil silhouettes (Juno's veil, Dorian's/Larch's silver hair).
  5. **Missing `SnapEdges = true` & void leaks:** `mapgen/layouts/tidelock.py:23` (`170-174`), Playhouse Backstage (`151-153`), and `522` Glassed Orchard (`docs/playtest/21_glassed_orchard.png`) omit `SnapEdges = true` (violating `design/GYM_PUZZLES.md:227`), and `120` Briarwick's south warp edge (`docs/playtest/28_briarwick.png`) lacks a south border skirt. Also on `171` Low-Water Tidelock Gym, piers meet mud flush on W/E/N with invisible `passguard.blocker` walls (add a 1-tile stone kerb lip + label Wheels W/E/C with down/up arrows and single-press turn).
  6. **Minor visual/map polish still open:** Sideways/lying NPC sprites in Ferrow's Landing (`100`, seen in `portraits_larch.png`) and Pell Playhouse front row (`154`, `gym1_house_empty_good_full.png`); Meadow oak trunk clipped behind scrolling cloud strip (`36_j1_on_the_promenade_green_meadow.png`); War Climax cutscene `pfw_fx_glass_*` still rendered in retired Shattered Ice blue instead of bottle-green trinitite + amber seams (`war_climax_dunes_engine_sheet.png`).
