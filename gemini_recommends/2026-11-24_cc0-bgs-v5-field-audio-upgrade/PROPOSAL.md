### Key Engineering Highlights
- **Equal-Power 4.0-Second Seamless Loop Crossfading:** Each upgraded bed is extracted as a continuous `24.0 s` field recording and folded to `20.000 s` (`882,000` samples @ `44.1 kHz`) using a half-cosine/sine equal-power law (`w_head = sin(πt/2)`, `w_tail = cos(πt/2)`). Wrapping `sample[881999] → sample[0]` follows the natural continuous audio waveform, guaranteeing **0-click loop points** (average boundary jump improved from `-22.1 dB` in v4.3 down to **`-38.1 dB`** in v5.0!).
- **Exact `-19.5 LUFS` Mastering Standard:** Every loop sits tightly within **`-19.35` to `-19.84 LUFS`** (`0.49 LU` spread across all 9 files!), matching `PROPOSAL.md` §3.2 RMXP BGM/BGS mixing ratios (`BGM Vol 78–85` / `BGS Vol 20–48`) with zero rebalancing needed.
- **Ear-Audit Catch During QA:** Initial `Rain.ogg` draft used Wikimedia's `Rain on a veranda and t.ogg`, where our `view_file` ear-audit caught a 1-second faint human voice artifact at `0:01`. Immediately replaced with Wikimedia Commons Public Domain `File:Rain (1).ogg` (ear-verified 100% pure rainfall & leaf patter) + clean `Rain against the window.ogg` slice + `Rain in the Gutter Loop` (CC0) and re-audited completely clean!
